## What's new

### ✨ New
- 👶 **Kids profile: a kids profile gets a short menu (Home, Live TV, Guide, Movies, Series) and no Settings or More. Put a PIN on the adult profiles to close the way back**
- 🏀 **Liga ACB and EuroLeague: season calendar, results, live quarter and table**
- 🗂️ **Choose several playlists at once: tick them in the picker, and Live, Guide and Home follow**
- 🗂️ **Playlists as a row of chips under the category search; ◀ ▶ over the chips switches list without OK**
- 🗂️ **Category sheet: the channels of the focused category appear beside it with what is on now**
- 🙈 **Hide Recent, Favourites or a playlist with a long press on OK; "Show hidden" brings it back**
- 🔁 **Channel failover: a channel that will not play is swapped for the same channel elsewhere in its playlist**
- 🔁 **Lists wrap around: ▲ on the first channel jumps to the last, ▼ on the last to the first**
- 📺 **Live player: channel and programme on top, all controls in one full-width row**
- 🪟 **Poster strip: a programme window on the right with title, time, progress and what comes next**
- 🖼️ **Channel panel: widescreen programme picture, then the channel logo, then just the text**
- 🔄 **Automatic sync between your paired devices, with a switch to turn it off**
- 📺 **Watch on TV: send a channel, film or series from your phone to your TV**
- 🎤 **Mic button in TV search (only if the TV has voice recognition)**
- 🎞️ **Emby: what you already watched on the server shows up here, with ✓ marks, progress bars and Continue watching**
- 🎞️ **Trailers in your language first, when there is one**
- 🆕 **"New" label on movies and series added in the last week**
- ✅ **✓ on the poster of a series you have watched to the end**
- 🧹 **Hold OK on Continue and Recent cards to remove them; "Clear favorites" in Favorites**
- 🏷️ **Platform logos: wide by default, square icons as an option; studio name when it has no logo**
- 🧭 **Guide matches channels like "ES-4 La 1 HD" by itself**
- 🏟️ **Watch live: only channels airing the match, no more "usually airs" rows**
- 🎯 **ExoPlayer opens multi-quality channels on the best quality**
- 🔀 **Based on OwnTV 5.2.1: guide preview on OK, audio delay per output, playback fixes**

### 🔧 Fixes
- 🔊 **ExoPlayer: the sound no longer drops a few seconds in when auto frame rate switches the display**
- ⚡ **Zapping: guide pictures, posters and the schedule column appear faster**
- 🎯 **Categories: ◀ ▶ across the playlist chips no longer drops the cursor to Favourites**
- 🎯 **Home: the Sports row rests at its first card instead of being cut at the edge**
- 🧩 **Sync page: the PIN and QR card fits on screen**
- 🔢 **Episode numbers readable on light themes**
- 🖼️ **Zap panel shows the same picture as the guide**
- 📋 **Guide: wider channel-name column so names are no longer cut**
