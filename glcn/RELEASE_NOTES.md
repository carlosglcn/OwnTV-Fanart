## What's new — bug-fix update

### 🔧 Fixes
- 🎬 **Films and series get their cast, title lettering, genres and trailers back.** The previous version's trailer-language change made the saved TMDB details of every title with a trailer expire, and the clean-up after the next playlist sync deleted them. Incomplete titles are now cleared once and fetched again in full as you browse, and a failed fetch never replaces full details again
- 💡 **Recommended: your own free TMDB key** (Settings › Metadata › Metadata source › Your TMDB key; "Get the key from another device" lets you send it from your phone). With it there is no daily allowance, and everything comes back at once instead of little by little
- 🧯 **No more freezes or closes while syncing several playlists:** on TV boxes the playlists now sync one after another, and the app asks Android for more memory headroom
- 🐢 **Your own TMDB key is paced** (about five calls a second) instead of asking for hundreds of titles at once
- 🪟 **Channel options over the player** are nearly solid, so the card behind no longer mixes with the menu
- 📝 **TV Home:** Recently watched shows the programme's synopsis
- 🏷️ **"[DV R]" reads Dolby Vision · REMUX** on the title's page
- 🏎️ **Sports and leagues and Customize lists** scroll as fast as the highlight
- ⚡ **Lighter on modest boxes:** the TV guide keeps fewer rows in memory, seasonal particles and the aurora are lighter, and text matching is prepared once

### ✨ Also new
- ✏️ **Category icons:** a line icon for each group in the category lists (sport, cinema, each genre…), the country code and the quality mark — hold OK on a group › Icon to change it (Settings › Fanart › Category icons)
- 🎭 **Programme info:** the cast moves to the right, so the synopsis has the whole left column
- 🫧 **Elastic motion that keeps up:** single steps land with a small give; held keys glide quickly so the screen never falls behind

### 🙏 Thank you
Thank you to everyone in the community who reports bugs and shares ideas — many of the fixes in this version come from your messages.
