## Beta

Versión de pruebas de OwnTV Fanart. Se instala al lado de la normal.
- 🎨 Player channel list: the card under the picture follows the theme — light with the light theme; with the dark one it takes a deep tone from the programme's own picture (navy when the picture has no colour). The programme's picture on its right is veiled just as much as its brightness needs for every word over it to read, so bright pictures stay readable and dark ones stay seen; a white or near-black accent switches to the theme's ink there.
- 🎵 Player channel list: the channel you are watching is shown clearly — a soft accent wash over its row and small moving bars by its name (instead of a thin line by the number); when you scroll it out of sight, a "▲ Cuatro · watching now" pill shows at the edge it went past.
- ‹ Player channel list: a slim tab on the list's left edge, with a ‹, nudges as the list opens — the categories are there with ◀.
- 🔘 Programme info (ⓘ): the buttons are real buttons now (glass, the first one tinted) and ▼ lands on the first; on another channel's page a "Watch" button tunes it.
- ⚽ Programme info on a match the Sports board knows: both crests with their names, the score and the minute, under the title (the score hidden with spoiler-free on).
- 🖼️ Programme info: the shade over the picture is as strong as that picture needs for the words to read and no stronger — pale pictures under the light theme are no longer washed out.
- 💬 Messages raised outside a screen (a playlist about to expire, a category hidden, a channel that won't open, the sleep timer that can't turn the screen off, Labs) show in the app's own glass pill instead of Android's grey toast.
- 🗓️ Guide: CH+ / CH− turn the guide a day forward / back, at the same time of day.
- 🎨 White or very pale accents (and near-black ones in the dark theme): titles, headings, times and marks drawn in the accent switch to the theme's text colour where the accent would vanish on the page — across Settings, the Guide, Sports, Home, Live TV, popups and the info screens.
- 🔍 A film's page opened from Películas (or the Runway) and a series opened from Series grow into place, as a title opened from Home already did.
- 🌈 Programme info (ⓘ): the page takes a soft glow in the picture's own tone, as the film and series pages already did.
- 🔤 Big titles — a film's or series' name without title art, Home's featured title, the Cine banner and Programme info — use a display face (Oswald): taller and narrower, so long names fit and read as headlines. With another app font chosen in Settings, that font is kept.
- 🪟 Programme info: its panels float a little above the page (a soft shadow), the first of three set heights — page, card, raised.
- 🌅 Empty pages (no favourites, no history, an empty guide) show a small sunset drawing with the page's icon over their words.
- 🏷️ Light theme: white one-colour channel logos (Telecinco…) are drawn in the theme's text colour instead of vanishing on the light page — their own shape, no plate; logos with colour and the dark theme are unchanged.
- 🪟 Player channel list, light theme: the card under the picture is the list's own glass colour, so both read as one sheet.
- 🎨 Player channel list: the playlist chips at the top (Todas · Movistar · Emby) wear each playlist's own colour when picked, as its tag does; "All" keeps the accent.
- › Player channel list: the categories panel has a slim tab with a › on its right edge, nudging as it opens — ▶ goes back to the channels.
- 👻 Ghost logos (behind live cards, in the Guide, on TV en directo's focused row): a white one-colour logo on the light theme (or a black one on the dark) no longer vanishes — it is drawn as its shape in the theme's text colour, still faint; other logos stay grey.
- 🎵 The quick channel lists over the player (the zapping list, Recent) mark the channel you are watching as the big list does: a soft accent wash with small moving bars instead of the thin line, and a "▲ La 1 UHD · watching now" pill when it is scrolled out of sight.
- 🏷️ The quick channel lists over the player draw the logos bare, like the big channel list — no glass tile behind each one; TV en directo's page keeps its tiles.
- ✂️ TV en directo: the focused row's "what's next" line is left out when it does not fit (a larger app font or interface size) instead of being drawn half off the row; the lines that fit are centred.
- 🎬 Cine banner: a long title takes two lines and shrinks to fit instead of ending in "…" after one word; the time line may wrap; "Up next" is narrower so the title has its room; a guide "synopsis" that is only facts ("Deportes,Fútbol | 2026 | TP | *5.6/10") is no longer shown as one (in every banner style).
- 🔠 TV en directo: with a larger text size (Settings › Fonts, e.g. 125%), the channel rows grow with it, so the focused row's three lines still fit.
- 🖼️ Cine banner: the programme's picture is smaller (about a quarter less wide), leaving the title more room.
- 🕰️ Player (live TV): the clock card in the corner is now a slim pill in the top centre — time · date · weather (icon, degrees, city) — and, only while they apply, the sleep timer's minutes left, "● Recording" and a reminder starting within 15 minutes ("In 5 min: … · channel"). The corners stay free for the channel's logo and the scoreboard; no more "Current time" label.
- 🌫️ Player (live TV): the shades behind the bars no longer veil the picture — a short, light one at the top for the pill, and the one at the foot starts lower; in the light theme the match no longer looks like it is in fog.
- 🎞️ Trakt: the app now signs in through its production Trakt app, so anyone can connect (the old one was limited to invited testers). Accounts connected before need to sign in again once.
- ‹ The quick channel list over the player has the same ‹ tab on its left edge (◀ opens the categories), and the categories a › tab on their right edge while their channels sit beside them (▶ goes there).
- ✨ Films and Series: a poster still on its way shows its shape, softly shimmering, instead of a gap; in the Guide a row whose programmes are still loading shows two shimmering cells.
- 🖼️ Programme info (ⓘ): what follows on the channel shows as picture cards under the buttons (the next one wider, its picture from the guide or TMDB); the right-hand list keeps only those after them, and the synopsis takes fewer lines to leave room.
- 🔘 Favourites, History, the playback report and Downloads: their actions (clear, export, share, watch setting, folder) are glass buttons instead of loose text with an icon.
- 🪟 Popups float a little higher than the page, with a soft shadow.

- 🏷️ Light theme: white one-colour logos are now a soft grey that keeps their own detail (an outline or inner line stays visible), instead of solid black.
- ℹ️ Programme info: the buttons are all the same glass (the faint tinted "Watch" looked disabled); the right-hand list is headed "Later", as "Up next" is now the picture cards.
- 🎞️ Trakt: "My Trakt watchlist" on Home also finds films by their name and year when the app has not matched them on TMDB yet (before, only TMDB-matched films showed, so the row could stay empty). Films whose playlist name is in another language than Trakt's title may still be missed.

- 🎨 Text on the accent colour always reads: with a pale accent (or a theme whose own text colour barely shows on it) the focused row's text turns near-black, and white on a dark accent.

- 🏷️ Light theme: white one-colour logos are a lighter grey, and the dark glow some of them carry (made for dark backgrounds) is faded out, so they no longer look blurred.
