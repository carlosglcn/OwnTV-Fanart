## Beta

Versión de pruebas de OwnTV Fanart. Se instala al lado de la normal.
- 📺 Player picture strip and programme info: when a guide lists two programmes at once, the floating card and the info screen name the same show as the card (the one that started last), and "Up next" follows it
- 🏃 Home: moving from card to card feels like the player's picture strip — the focus box is on the next card almost at once, with only a small give, instead of trailing behind and catching up
- 📺 Programme info: the channel goes on playing in a small window up on the right, so nothing is missed while you read
- 🔤 Programme info: the synopsis reads over bright pictures (deeper shade, a little narrower), the age rating is a badge of its own, and "Airs again this week" only shows when it does
- ⏪ Programme info: From the start (catch-up channels) and Record buttons beside Technical data
- 🗂️ Emby / Jellyfin: choose how a library is split into categories — by genre (now series too), by platform or network (Netflix, HBO…, from Emby's studios) or as one list each for films and series (Settings › Fanart › Emby / Jellyfin categories; applies at the next sync)
- 🧯 Emby / Jellyfin sync reads the library a page at a time instead of holding every episode (with its media streams) in memory at once — a big library filled the memory while syncing
- 🇪🇸 Emby / Jellyfin genres in Spanish when the app is in Spanish (Ciencia ficción, Miniseries, Documental…), so a library tagged in English and one in Spanish share their categories
- 🏷️ Emby / Jellyfin by platform: each platform shows its own logo (TMDB's network logo) in the category list; variants are joined (HBO Nordic → HBO, Paramount+ with Showtime → Paramount+, Sky One/Max/Atlantic → Sky…), networks with only one or two shows go in "Other networks", and films keep their genres (their studios are their makers, not where they stream)
- 🪄 Programme info opens with the picture folding into its window: the TV shrinks smoothly to its place on the right, its corners rounding, while the backdrop comes up around it (and the reverse on closing)
- 📺 TV profile: Settings no longer offers the Home layouts and Home backdrop — a TV profile always has its own TV Home
- ✨ New motion (off on modest boxes and in light mode): Settings pages slide in from the right; menus and windows grow in with a fade; entering a profile, its screen opens up towards you as Home comes in; the player's picture strip rises card after card; the programme bar fills as the player's card comes up
- ◀ ▶ Programme info walks the channels: ◀ ▶ (or CH±) show what is on the next channels of the list, sliding smoothly while the window keeps playing yours; ▼ goes to the buttons (◀ ▶ there between Record and Technical data) and ▲ back; OK tunes the channel shown. The channels either side are read ahead so each page is ready
- 🗓️ Guide: the provider's own short guide also names, with overlapping programmes, the one that started last — like every other screen
- 🧠 Memory: the now/next caches no longer grow with every channel ever focused, channel logos are fetched by a few workers (not one task per logo) and skipped in light mode, and Home reads the live channels once instead of twice
- 🧘 **Changing section (Home, Live TV, Sports…) is a clean cut:** the small sideways slide that read as a judder is gone, and pages ease out without going past
- 🎬 Emby / Jellyfin by platform: films from a streaming platform's own label (Netflix, Apple, Prime Video, Disney+, HBO Max…) go under that platform with its logo; the rest keep their genre
- 🏷️ **Category names: text, icons or logos** (Settings › Fanart › Category icons): words only by default; icons beside the name; or, for the platforms, their logo in place of the name. Logos drop the white plate and take the row's colour; "Other networks" gets an icon, and a platform no show carries finds its logo on TMDB's network page
- ↔️ **Move Home's rows:** in the TV profile's Home, hold OK on a row's name (Starting now, Films on air…) and move it with ◀ ▶; OK leaves it there. In the Dock, hold OK on a tab › Move row — same keys, and the order is the one in Settings › Home
- 🎞️ **Xtream: the film's own TMDB id and trailer from your list:** when the panel keeps them, the film or show is matched exactly (no guessing by name) and its trailer is used when TMDB has none
- 🐛 **TV Home: the programme panel showed the previous programme's synopsis** (a film's under a talk show) — read afresh for every programme now
- 🔎 **Emby / Jellyfin: no TMDB search by name** for what your server already identified — its own TMDB ids are kept at sync and used directly (fewer TMDB calls, no wrong match)
- 🖼️ Zapping › Coming up: the programme picture keeps its 16:9 shape (it was cut at the top and bottom)
- 🇪🇸 Title logos in your country's version: within Spanish, Spain's logo before Mexico's (TMDB files both as Spanish)
- 📺 **Live TV's bar, lower and lighter:** no panel any more — a deeper shade at the foot of the screen carries the text; smaller programme picture, no synopsis there (it is on the programme's Info screen), the stream chips in one small line, and on channels with an archive the timeline takes the place of the dividing line
- ⭕ **One loading sign:** a ring where the play button sits while the channel opens, which closes and turns into the play sign when the picture arrives (instead of a spinner on its own); with the bar up, the play button wears the ring
- ⚖️ **Platform logos at the same optical size:** each logo is sized by its own shape (about the same area, within a height and a width), so Netflix no longer looks huge next to BBC One or PBS
- 🧹 **No "EMBY" mark on the platform rows** — they can only come from Emby / Jellyfin
- 🔢 **Platforms from most titles to fewest**, "Other networks" always last (sync the list again once)
- 🏷️ **Playlist marks with the service's own logo** (Emby, Jellyfin, Plex, Movistar) when that artwork is in the app; its name otherwise
- 🪼 Jellyfin playlists show Jellyfin's own icon on their mark (jellyfin-ux, CC BY-SA 4.0)
- 📝 Zapping › Coming up: the synopsis gets up to six lines (the free room above the picture)
- 🎯 Zapping › Coming up: ▶ starts on the programme on air (the top row), not on the one after it
- 🟠 Plex playlists show Plex's own logo on their mark (used under Plex's trademark guidelines)
- 🌗 Plex's logo from Plex's own kit, in its version for dark grounds and for light ones (light theme, focused row)
- 🟢 Emby playlists show Emby's own logo (on a focused row or the light theme, its icon alone)
- 🔄 Merged the original OwnTV's latest: Stalker portals crawled more gently, with optional login fields, and playlist counts after the background download; builds on Java 25 / Gradle 9.8.2
- 📡 **Guide refresh, lighter:** an automatic refresh first asks the server whether the guide changed and skips an unchanged file (no download, no reading); on devices with room to spare (not low-RAM boxes) it no longer waits for you to stop watching. "Sync now" still reads it in full
- ⏸️ **Pause screen for films and episodes:** a few seconds into a pause, with the controls gone, what you are watching comes up over the picture — lettering, episode, facts, synopsis and cast. Any key brings the controls back
- ⏭️ **Next episode card with its picture** in the corner (the episode's still, or the show's fanart), the countdown filling along its foot
- 🔔 **TV Home: a reminder in one press** — on a programme still to come (Starting now, Tonight), OK reminds you of it (a bell shows; OK again forgets it) and holding OK watches the channel now
- 🔤 **Styled subtitles (ASS/SSA) shown as made:** a film or episode from Emby / Jellyfin that carries them starts on mpv, which draws their fonts, colours and positions (ExoPlayer showed plain text); not for Dolby Vision (sync the list once)
- 📖 **Chapters** for films with chapters (when mpv plays them): a Chapters button in the cinema banner and a tab in the side panel — the one playing marked, OK jumps to it
- 💿 **Discs copied as folders** on your network shares (SMB / NFS): a Blu-ray folder (BDMV) or a DVD folder (VIDEO_TS) shows as one film and plays its main title (on mpv)
- 📱 **Apps on TV Home:** an "Apps" pill at the end of the rows with the TV's other apps you pick (YouTube, Plex, Kodi…) as their own banners — OK opens one, hold OK takes it off, the last card adds more — hidden for now: only on a device where the test sequence (▲ ▼ four times in a row, in Settings) has switched it on
- 🏠 **TV profile Home: Full picture or Dock** — Settings › Design: a TV profile can now use the Dock (TV only: channels, sport, tonight) instead of the full-picture Home; just for that profile
- 🎯 Logos alone in the category list: a little larger and centred in the row (they sat at the left with the row empty to their right)
- 🧪 **Test features (▲ ▼ four times in Settings): Apps, the SHIELD way** — More › Apps: "Your apps" (picked, in order) over "All apps", every banner one size and framed, the focused one lifting and the page taking its colours; hold OK to add or take off, move, or open the app's Android settings. The "Add apps" picker on TV Home is now a dark grid of banners with a ✓ on the added ones (added first, with a count), and the row's Add card is small
- 🧪 **Test feature: open when the TV starts** — off by default; Settings › App (or More › Apps): OwnTV Fanart comes up on start-up and when the TV wakes; Android asks once for "Display over other apps"
- 📍 **Chapter marks on the film's seek bar** (thin cuts where each chapter starts, mpv)
- ⏩ **Skip intro from the chapters:** a file whose chapters name its intro ("Intro", "Opening"…) offers Skip intro — films too — and lands exactly where it ends (Auto-skip uses it as well)
- 🌈 **HDR on a TV without HDR:** an HDR / Dolby Vision film plays on mpv's GL path, which turns it to SDR with the right colours instead of washed-out ones (only when the screen takes no HDR at all)
- ⏱️ Zap timing in the diagnostics log: for each channel change, when the first data arrived and whether the provider redirected it, next to the decoder start and the first picture
- ⚡ Faster channel changes: live playback starts after half a second of buffer instead of one (about 0.3–0.5 s saved per zap; recovery after a stall still waits 2 s).
- 🪟 New setting in Appearance › Glass and background: "Panels over the video" — five levels, from very see-through to very solid, for the player and channel-change panels.
- 🎞️ Runway (Films and Series): coming back from a title or the player lands on the poster you left; CH+/CH− jump a screenful; a held ◀ no longer slips out to the menu; the row glides with less work for the TV.
- ⬆️ Top bar navigation: ▲ from the top of Sports, the Guide or any section now reaches the bar.
- 📰 Live TV (on trial): the column beside the channel list is now the programme's page — title, a longer synopsis (up to 6 lines) and what follows over the programme's picture, with the video small in the top right corner; key hints no longer sit on the schedule.
- 🌗 Live TV page: the picture darkens more behind the title and synopsis, so the text never sits on faces.
- 🖼️ Live TV page: bigger video, the programme's picture runs the whole column down, and four programmes follow instead of three.
- 📱 Apps (test feature): calmer page — no more pale wash behind white banners (a soft glow from the top instead), focused banners never clipped, more room between them, the start-up switch moved to Settings › App only, and a hint for hold-OK options.
- 🖼️ Live TV page: the title runs across the top, the video is as wide as the text beside it, and the programme's picture sits whole at the bottom, fading out upwards.
- 📺 Live TV page: the live video is bigger (60% of the column) with the synopsis beside it and what follows across the full width; a programme listed twice by the guide shows once.
- 🖼️ Player bar: the programme's picture is half as big again.
- 🃏 Zapping strip: five whole cards across (none cut at the edge), a little narrower and squarer (3:2).
- 🃏 Zapping strip: the row is clipped at its margins, so no sliver of the next card shows at the edge.
- 📺 Live TV page: the live video across the whole column at the top; title, details, a 4-line synopsis and what follows (in one row) underneath, with the programme's picture showing at the bottom.
- 🎞️ Chapters tab: picture cards two across — each chapter's frame (Emby/Jellyfin chapter images when the server has them, otherwise read from the film in the background and kept for next time; the film's backdrop with the number when neither is possible), "Chapter 01" shown as "Capítulo 1", and the one playing with its progress.
- 🧊 Fix: no trailer player is started for a few seconds after closing a film, nor while Android reports low memory — starting one then could freeze the app ("not responding").
- 🧠 Emby/Jellyfin sync uses far less memory: the library is written to a file as it is read instead of being held whole in memory (it pushed the TV into "critical memory" during a sync).
- 🩺 Memory diary in the playback report: once a minute, off the main thread, a line when memory moves noticeably (or every 10 min), with what was going on — playing, trailer, picture cache, syncing.
- 🐢 Stall watch: if the app stops answering for 3 s, what it was doing is written to the report (only while it is on screen).
- 🛑 Memory brake: past a limit set by the device's memory (~490 MB on a SHIELD), the pictures kept in memory, the trailer, picture prefetch and chapter frames are let go — never what is playing; light mode is never switched on. All of it on one sleeping low-priority thread, unnoticeable even in long sessions.
- 🩹 Live TV page: what follows is always on screen under the big video — the synopsis gives way (and drifts) instead of pushing it under the key hints; the title is one line there.
- 🧯 Trailer freezes: when the trailer's web page stops answering, it is ended at once (Android 10+) and the trailer let go, instead of the whole app freezing until Android closes it.
- 📋 Playback report: the diagnostic lines come from the saved file, so what happened just before a freeze or a crash is in the report.
- 🎯 Runway: coming back from a film's or a show's page keeps the focus on that title instead of jumping to the first.
- 🖼️ Live TV page: until the video plays (or with the preview off) the video box shows the programme's picture instead of a black frame with the channel number; the picture at the bottom is quieter so the text over it reads.
- 👋 Who's watching: easier to read (darker over the picture, bigger subtitle), "Add profile" the same size and shape as the profiles (dashed, with a "+"), names on one line and the group centred with equal gaps, the focused profile with a thick rim and a glow in its colour, and a plain clock (time and date).
- 🪶 Who's watching: smaller and lighter — smaller profiles, names and greeting, a softer glow, and "Add profile" as a small button under the profiles instead of a big tile.
- 🌫️ Live TV page: behind the column, the programme's picture as a soft blur of its colours instead of a second copy of the image.
- 🔎 Live TV page: a programme the guide has no picture for is looked up by its title (once, cached) for the video box; with nothing found, the channel's logo sits small in the middle.
- 🌒 Guide: the programme's picture darkens towards its foot, so the synopsis and key hints over it read.
- 🎬 Trailers behind a title: while the trailer plays the still picture goes away (no more band at the side or another scene under it), the video's edges melt into the page and the page's tint steps aside meanwhile.

- 🐇 Trailers lighter: they play only once a film's or show's page is open (no longer for every title the cursor rests on in the list), start after 4 s on the page, and the trailer player is let go 10 s after leaving instead of a minute.
- 🧭 Memory diary also shows how much memory the whole box has free (the trailer's web page and other apps never show in the app's own figure), and the memory brake also steps in when the box runs short.
- 🧩 Apps (test feature): holding OK on an app opens its menu and it stays open — the release of the same press no longer chose the first entry.
- 🚀 Right after the box restarts: "Open when the TV turns on" waits 40 s before opening the app, and the start-up syncs (playlists, guide) wait until the box has been up 3 minutes — Android is busiest and shortest of memory then, and the app froze at times.
- ✨ Live TV facelift: every logo on one rounded glass tile of the same size, quieter channel numbers, the programme without the cut-off "time left" (now a small ring with the minutes in it), the focused row also showing what comes next, no playlist mark on every row when all channels come from one playlist, group names without web addresses or "@", icon-only tools that show their name on focus, a soft ambient light from the programme across the whole screen, and key hints that show for a few seconds and then step aside.
- 🔲 Live TV: logos sit inside a visible glass tile with room around them (all read the same size), and the ambient light behind the list is a true blur (the sharp picture showed through).
- 🧱 Live TV, second pass: narrower category column with smaller names, lower and more compact channel rows (smaller logo tile, name and ring), the live-match count beside the sports icon ("⚽ 2"), a calmer programme page (smaller title, time left with the times, what follows as glass chips), and room at the foot of the list.
- 🎞️ Trailers smoother: a trailer is prepared and started only once the remote has rested a moment (moving through the page no longer loads it), the trailer player stays warm between pages so the next one starts quickly (the memory brake still lets it go when memory is short), and it waits paused while the lower part of the page (cast, versions) is open.
- 🖼️ Live TV: when the guide has no picture for a programme, the one found by its title now also lights the screen's backdrop, not only the video box.
- 🎬 Title page trailer: it starts about 3 seconds after the page opens, whether you move around it or not; when it ends, the page goes back to its usual backdrop.
- ⚡ Lighter trailers: the trailer player is drawn at a smaller size and scaled up to its window, so YouTube sends a light picture (~540p) and the box decodes and draws a quarter of the pixels — smoother pages, less memory; behind the page's soft edges it looks the same.
- 📈 Smoothness meter: the playback report now says, minute by minute, how smoothly each screen was drawn (frames, how many missed the 60 fps beat, the worst one and whether a trailer was playing) — so the slow spots are fixed by measurement.
- 🚀 Fanart's own screens are now compiled ahead on the box (they were missing from the app's start-up profile): fewer stutters on the first scrolls after installing — Android applies it in the background, so it shows fully after a while.
- 📺 Live TV (Ficha): no more picture spread over the whole screen — the programme's picture now sits only behind the text under the video, softly blurred and dimmed; the video is a little smaller and the synopsis a little smaller too, so many more lines read at once.

- 📋 Channel list in the player: no playlist mark on each row when every channel comes from one list; the right column now shows the programme first (picture, title, time, a longer synopsis) and the evening's schedule under it — no empty gap, and no repeated "Next" line.
- 📝 Drifting synopses always rest on whole lines (the first line could show cut at the top).

- 🪧 Compact live banner: a programme without a picture shows the same short card (it showed a taller one with the synopsis).
- 🎬 Title pages: the trailer now plays in its own larger window at the top left, above the title, and the picture stays whole behind; if a tall logo or a long synopsis leaves too little room there, it plays in the picture as before.
- 🎬 Title page trailer window: nothing shows there until the video really plays (an empty frosted frame stood there while it loaded), no clipping around the video (it played badly or not at all), and a slow first start is given 15 s before it is skipped.
