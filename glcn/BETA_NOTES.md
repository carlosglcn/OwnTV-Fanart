## Beta

Versión de pruebas de OwnTV Fanart. Se instala al lado de la normal.

- 📐 Player channel list: set the width of groups, channels and programme in Settings › Layout › Panel widths (new "Player channel list" row). The groups fold to their icons while you browse the channels, and the programme column takes that room.
- 🪟 Player channel list: the programme column is as solid as the list, so the schedule no longer mixes with the picture or its subtitles.
- 📺 Cine and Ficha banners: the stream's facts (resolution, Hz, HDR…) are shown next to the channel, as in the other styles.
- 🖼️ Cine banner: the programme's picture beside the title.
- 🎮 Remote shortcuts: new "Programme info" action — opens the programme on air in full (picture, synopsis, what follows) with one button.
