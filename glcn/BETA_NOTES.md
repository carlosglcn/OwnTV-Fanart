## Beta

Versión de pruebas de OwnTV Fanart. Se instala al lado de la normal.
- 🔄 Merged the original app's latest (Nov 19–21): catch-up decodes in hardware without dropped frames and reopens by itself when a programme drops them, a per-playlist "Prefer HLS for catch-up" switch, subtitles from a folder ("Search in folder", best match first, optional auto-load), search and A–Z in every alphabet, a notice while the database is converted after an update, per-profile text sizes and recent searches, voice search with the default app, Ukrainian and Central Kurdish. Thanks, ahXN00.
- ⏳ Settings › Playlists: a playlist waiting its turn to sync says "Queued" instead of "Syncing" — with several updating at once they all read the same and looked stuck.
- 🔎 Player channel list: a quick search. ▲ from the first channel reaches a magnifier beside the list's name; OK opens it with the TV's keyboard, and what you type finds channels in every active playlist as you write — each result says which group it is in, OK tunes it and zapping goes on in that group. Back puts the search away. (▲ on the first channel no longer jumps to the last one.)
- 📌 Live TV: an optional channel list with fixed focus (Settings › Layout › "Channel list with fixed focus", off by default) — the highlight stays at one height and the channels pass under it, as in TiviMate or Apple TV; near the first and last channels it walks to them.
- 🔄 Merged the original app's latest (Nov 21 evening): a channel's **Archive** page with its past days and their programmes, catch-up on Flussonic servers from the VOD playlist, the catch-up guide fills from the playlist when the feed is stale, guide matches re-sync the feed when needed, a Player buttons option for what ▲ ▼ do in the live player (zap, controls and recent channels, or nothing), hold OK / Menu on Home's trending posters, Downloads opens on Recordings, the Info key opens Program details in Live TV. Thanks, ahXN00.
- 🎨 **Posters and backdrops in full colour:** pictures were kept with fewer colours to save memory, which showed as grain and banding in dark posters; now only low-RAM boxes and Light mode keep the saving
- 🔤 **Crisper small type on 720p screens:** many budget TVs draw apps at 720p, where small bold text came out jagged; there it is drawn a step lighter
- 🧹 **No channel watermark in the Guide:** the faint channel logo behind the programme's words is gone
- 🔥 **A new style, Brasa:** warm graphite, an accent that runs from amber to coral, and an outlined focus — a focused row, card or poster is framed in the gradient instead of filled, so the picture under it still shows; buttons and menu items fill with the gradient. First in the style list, so a new install opens on it; who already has the app is offered it once ("Try it"), with a second question to keep it or go back.
- 🔠 Home's row labels (and "Keep watching" in Films and Series) are small spaced capitals instead of heavy titles: the page reads more ordered and the pictures keep the weight. In every style.
- 📚 **Films and Series: a new layout, Shelves** (Settings › Layout, with the others) — Cinematic, with rows above the posters of All: **Popular now** (the titles of Home's Trending that your lists carry), **Recent releases** (this year's, the latest added first) and **Best rated**. Each title once even when a list has it in several qualities; a row with fewer than four titles is left out. Wide pictures where the titles have them, posters where they do not. The section opens on the first shelf, OK and hold OK do what they do on a poster, and coming back from a title returns to its shelf. All the posters follow below as always.
- 🌅 **A light style with the gradient, Alba:** Brasa's sister for daytime — warm ivory, an accent from orange to raspberry and the same outlined focus.
- 🔔 **Menu sounds: a new style, Ember ("Brasa")** — warm and low: a soft knock to move, a rising fifth for OK, the same falling away for Back.
- 🧭 **Top bar: five shapes to choose from** (Settings › Layout › "Top bar shape"; each press moves on to the next and the bar above changes at once). **Capsule** is the bar as it was. **Island** sits in the middle with no background until the focus is on it — the open section carries its icon and a line in the accent, the logo stays at the left. **Tabs**: bare names in larger type over a soft veil, the open one underlined. **Pills**: every section its own chip, the open one light. **Icons**: a short capsule of icons that name themselves when open or focused. Who already had the app keeps the capsule; a new install opens on the island.
- 🧭 **Side menu: four shapes to choose from** (Settings › Layout › "Side menu shape", each press moves on to the next). **Glass** is the menu as it was. **Fade**: the glass thins out into the picture, the open section is marked with a bar in the accent, the focused row is outlined and — once the focus rests on it — shows a larger picture of what waits there; the profile rides beside the logo. **Column**: names alone, large, no icons or counts. **Label**: the menu stays closed and the focused icon says its name and count in a label beside it. Who already had the app keeps Glass; a new install opens on Fade.
- 🩹 Brasa and Alba: on a focused Settings row the value at its right (the chosen option, the switch, the NEW / FANART mark) vanished into the outlined row — it now keeps its own colours there. Side menu "Label": the label no longer stays behind when the focus goes on to the profile.
- 🏟️ **Sports: match cards in the teams' colours.** Each card takes its two sides' colours, read from their crests — home's from the left, away's from the right: deep tones under a dark style, a pale wash of the colours under a light one (races and players keep the plain card). A match about to start says how long is left ("in 29 min · 21:30") in the accent for its last three hours. And when a channel was already found for a match (its page was opened before), the card's foot names it instead of the stadium.
- 🎬 **TV Guide: filter by type.** A new "Type" button in the Guide's header: Everything, or only the channels showing a film, sport, a series, a documentary, the news or something for children **right now**, each with how many channels show it. It works together with the category and the order. The kind comes from what the guide says of each programme; when the last one of a kind ends, the filter lets go by itself.
- 🕒 Date, time & weather colours: a colour picked by hand that would not read under the current style (a pale one on a light style, a dark one on a dark style) gives way to the style's own colour there; the pick is kept and comes back with a style where it reads.
- 🩹 Light styles (Alba, Lumen Día, Nieve…): the player's LIVE badge was pale red words on a pale red wash and could not be read on the light banner — the words are now a deep red there.
- 🎽 Sports cards, the colours put right: a side's colour is now **its own** — the one its feed gives for it (its shirt's), not whichever colour was strongest in its crest, which put yellow on Barcelona and blue on Villarreal. Black-and-white sides read as silver or near black; the crest only stands in where the feed gives no colour (the first time, cards may still show the old colours until the scores refresh).
- 🎾 **Tennis: the surface.** A tennis card says what the tournament is played on — clay, grass, hard court, indoor hard court — after its name, and takes that surface's colour (orange, green, blue, violet). Told from the tournament's name; one that is not on the list shows nothing.
- 🏆 Sports cards: the **competition's own logo** before its name — LaLiga, the Premier League, the Champions League… — as the scores' feed gives it (its version for a dark background under a dark style). Tennis shows the **tournament's own logo** for the big ones (the Grand Slams, the Masters 1000, the Finals, the Davis Cup…), from our own data file, and the tour's (ATP, WTA) for the rest.
- 🎽 Sports cards, the colours rethought: the card itself is plain again, and each side carries **a bar of its own colour beside its crest** — vivid, as it is, never dulled into a background (Villarreal's yellow had turned mustard). Tennis players carry their tournament's surface colour.
- ⚡ **Brisk response** (Settings › Layout, on by default): everything that moves after a key — the focus sliding to its new place, lists and the Home page gliding, menus and pages opening — now takes half the time it did, and the menu opens the section the focus rests on after 0.2 s instead of 0.4. The slow ambient motion (pictures drifting) is untouched. Switch it off to get the gentler pace back and compare.
- 🏍️ **MotoGP: standings and results.** A MotoGP session's page now has the **riders' championship** (the Table tab, with points) and, once a session has run — practice, qualifying, the sprint, the race — its **result**: every rider in order, with the winner's time and the others' gap. Both come from the championship's own results feed.
- 🩹 Sports: the scores saved before the colours and logos came kept their old look (colours guessed from the crests — yellow for Barcelona, blue for Villarreal — and no league logo) until the board was next rebuilt; the saved board is now rebuilt once after updating.
- 🎾 Tennis cards: no colour bars beside the players (players have no colours of their own); the court's surface is now a small tag at the card's foot, in that surface's colour. Tournament logos are now served from our own data, so they load.
- 🏆 Competition logos for the leagues whose scores come without one — Liga ACB, EuroLeague, EuroCup, Liga ASOBAL, the EHF cups, the Bundesliga and other handball leagues, MotoGP, Formula 1, IndyCar, NASCAR, the UCI WorldTour, snooker — from our own data file.
- 🎨 Two sides in much the same colour (two reds, Burgos – Albacete) no longer look alike: the away side shows its second colour.
- 🏟️ **Match page:** the competition's logo beside its name at the top, each side's colour under its crest, how long until kick-off (its last three hours), and the court's surface for tennis. Before kick-off, the page opens on what there is (form, table…) instead of an empty minute-by-minute. Follow moved up beside the competition's name, so every tab fits (the last one, Broadcasts, was cut to "R…"); the tabs scroll if there are more than fit.
- 🎨 Style picker: Brasa and Alba have a group of their own, **Outlined focus**, first — they were among the Classic ones.
- ✨ A new install now opens on Fanart's own look: the menu as a top bar (Island), Home in Cinema and Films & Series in Runway, with Brasa. Whoever already has the app keeps every setting exactly as it was.
- 🎞️ Changing section (Live TV, Sports, the Guide, Home…) — the new one now comes in with a longer, softer settle instead of landing at once, and keeps that pace with Brisk response on (which only quickens what follows each key). The same for screens that build up piece by piece.
- 🩹 **Sports: a match or league page no longer opens empty** — in the first minutes after the app starts (or after an update) the page waited behind the guide's matching and showed nothing; it now opens at once and its channels fill in when they are found.
- 🏷️ Sports cards: tournament and competition logos show straight away when the app starts on its saved scores (they only came with the first refresh).
- 🧾 Playback report: the diary of the last minutes comes before the list of live groups, so a report shortened where it is sent still carries it; a match page slow to open is noted there.
- 🏷️ Match page: the competition's logo stays beside its name (it was lost when the match was read again).
- 🏟️ **Sports: where it is played, beyond football** — basketball and handball matches (ACB, EuroLeague, EuroCup, Primera FEB, Lega, ASOBAL, EHF, Bundesliga, LNH…) show the home side's arena, and the big tennis tournaments their ground, on the card and on the match page. The arenas are looked up once per club; a few may still be missing or need correcting.
- 🔊 **Brasa sounds, less synthetic:** rebuilt as a struck wooden bar — overtones that fade at their own pace, a faint shimmer, the mallet's soft thud, a little room around it — and moving through a list plays four slightly different takes instead of one identical click.
- 🔊 Brasa sounds: its own opening tone (four struck bars rising, with "Sound on opening" on) and a clearer move sound — brighter, almost dry, a touch higher; it sounded shut in.
- 🩹 Sports: the match page no longer waits for the settings and the playlists to be read again when it opens (slow right after the app starts, while it syncs) — it starts from what the section already knows.
- 🔊 Brasa: the move sound is now a short dry tap — no low thud, no second voice, no room — and a new one cuts the one before instead of ringing over it.
- 🩹 **Sports: opening another match shows that match** — the page could keep showing the one opened before (or stay empty) while the app was syncing: its channel search, slow to answer and to let go then, held the page back. The search now runs apart and the page changes at once.
- 🎚️ Channel lists: the playing channel's moving bars sit inside the row, over it, and in the ink that reads on a focused row — they were half hidden under the row's edge.
- 📺 **Guide: your playlist's own channel ids are kept** — the silent match after a list update could replace a channel's tvg-id with a look-alike name (M+ showed Telecinco, two La Séptima got La 1 and La 2). It now leaves channels with their own id alone and only applies near-certain name matches with the same numbers. A wrong match already saved goes with «Clear match» on that channel.
- 🩹 **History keeps its place** — a channel opened from History (the player's right-hand list or the recent-channels drop) now keeps History as its list: ◀ opens History on that channel and CH± steps through it, instead of the category watched before.
- 🔔 Brasa sounds open the app with their own tone without touching anything: "Sound on opening" is on with that pack until you set the switch yourself (the other packs still open in silence).
- ⭐ **Sports: follow a tennis player, a driver or a rider** — the "My teams" search in Sports settings now finds the players of the ATP and WTA rankings and the drivers and riders of F1 and MotoGP by name. In a race's page, OK on anyone in the session's order follows them (a star marks them); the hold-OK menu of a race lists the ones you follow and the first three.
- 🏁 A followed driver or rider: every session of their series counts as yours (My teams filter, Home row, alerts), the race card shows their place while it runs and when it ends, and the player's pill shows it live ("Márquez · P3").
- 🎨 **Appearance, simplified (Settings › Fanart › Appearance):** three choices — **Theme** (eight, each with a night and a day side), **Mode** (Automatic: light by day, dark from dusk; Dark; Light) and **Background** (Plain, the theme's own, or Fanart: the colours of the picture in focus, now with any dark theme). Accent, focus and sounds wait under **Personalise**; the styles of earlier versions under their own row. What you had stays as it was until you choose.
- 🌊 **New theme Ría:** deep navy with soft lights drifting behind, a cyan-to-green accent and outlined focus by night; a pale blue-grey page with navy ink by day. Estudio and Neón get a day side; Alba is now Brasa's day side, Nieve Aurora's, Arena Crepúsculo's, Papel Vitrina's.
- 🧊 Themes' surfaces take the theme's own tone (navy panels in Ría, warm ones in Brasa) instead of the same grey for all.
- 🩹 Sound style list: the focused name reads on the focus (it was the focus's own colour, invisible with light accents such as Ría's).
- 🎨 The theme shows beyond its focus: section labels in menus and sheets and Home's row names take the theme's colour (Ría's green by night, navy by day; Brasa's coral…), and the open section in the top bar (Capsule and Icons shapes) is a pill filled with the theme's gradient.
- 🧭 **More, tidier:** Settings is a wide button of its own under the profile; the group names are larger and in the theme's colour, with more room between rows; the backup says how long ago it was ("10 days ago", in amber past a month); the error log shows a number only for the last day's errors, in amber.
- ℹ️ **About in three blocks:** the version with its buttons right under it (Check for updates first), the community (both Telegram groups) and the credits (the original project and this version, wallpapers, Trakt's notice).
- ☕ Ko-fi: a third tile in About › Community (QR to scan with the phone), and a small Ko-fi code beside the star card's own — never a prompt of its own.
- 🔔 The reminder card (a programme you set or usually watch is starting) stays 8 seconds, at its real length — with Brisk response it was gone in under three. The channel-number countdown bar keeps its real length too.
- 🫧 **Top bar folds while you browse** (after iOS's tab bar): once you are in the content, the bar draws in around the open section, leaving the picture more room; ▲ opens it whole again. The Floating bar still hides completely.
- ✨ **Cards with a glass edge** (after iOS 27): a thin catch of light runs along the top of the focused card, landing on the side you came from and settling at the upper left; posters and cards get a soft darker rim along their foot, and glass panels too, so they stand off bright backgrounds.
- 🪟 Appearance › Personalise: a Glass entry takes you to the glass's transparency (Glass & background).
- 🟢 "EN DIRECTO" and the live dot take Ría's green by night (red as before in the other themes).
- 🌊 Ría Día: the focus outline runs from navy to a petrol blue instead of green, which clashed with the navy.
- 🏍️ MotoGP: a session's page has a **Weekend** tab with every session of the Grand Prix — day, time, live or done — the one you opened highlighted.
- 🔄 A switched-off playlist's own guide no longer downloads at start-up (its guide kept its own Auto refresh).
- 🤾 Handball Bundesliga and the Hungarian league get their logos.
- 🗂️ Shelves layout (Films and Series): two more rows — **Added this week** (what your list dated in the last seven days) and **In 4K** (titles marked 4K/UHD, or filed in a 4K group).
- ✅ **Episodes are marked watched in the end credits:** leaving in the last 7 % (never under 1:30), or pressing Play now / letting the countdown start the next one, now counts — and from where the credits really start when the file's chapters or TheIntroDB say; before only the last 5 % did, and going on to the next episode could leave it unwatched.
- 📺 **Emby/Jellyfin → Trakt:** an episode or film OwnTV counts as watched (now in the end credits) is marked watched on the Emby server itself, so Emby's Trakt plugin adds it to your history — before, Emby only marked it past 90 % of a stopped report, which leaving in the credits or going on to the next episode never reached. Jellyfin too (its reports now carry the header newer Jellyfin asks for).
