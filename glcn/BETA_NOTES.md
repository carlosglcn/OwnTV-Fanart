## Beta

Versión de pruebas de OwnTV Fanart. Se instala al lado de la normal.
- 🎞️ **Trailers in your language first, when there is one**
- ⚡ **Guide pictures appear faster when zapping**
- 🔄 **Automatic sync between your paired devices while the app is open**
- 🔘 **Switch to turn automatic sync on or off**
- 📺 **Watch on TV: send a channel, film or series from your phone to your TV**
- 🧩 **Sync page: PIN and QR card now fits on screen**
- 🏷️ **Platform logos: wide by default, square service icons as an option**
- ⚡ **Platform logo appears faster**
- 🏷️ **Studio name shown when it has no logo**
- 🧭 **Guide matches channels like “ES-4 La 1 HD” or “ES-3 La 1 SD” by itself, on TV and phone**
- 🗂️ **Playlists are a row of chips right under the category search**
- 🙈 **Long-press OK on Recent, Favourites or a playlist to hide it; “Show hidden” brings it back**
- ⚡ **Posters while zapping: no stale poster, and the whole stretch around the cursor is preloaded**
- 📺 **Live player: channel and programme (with poster) on top, all controls in one full-width row**
- 🏟️ **Watch live: only channels airing the match (or about to) — no more “usually airs” rows**
- 🖼️ **Zap panel shows the same programme picture as the guide (no mismatched TMDB image)**
- 🎬 **Home Cinema: rows fade out softly instead of a hard cut when scrolled; Movies/Series use the cover when a title has no wide picture**
- 🖼️ **Live list: bigger channel logos and the programme poster beside its info**
- 🆕 **“New” label on movies and series added in the last week**
- 🎤 **Mic button in TV search: speak with the SHIELD remote (shown only if the TV has voice recognition)**
