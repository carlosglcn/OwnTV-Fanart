## Beta

Versión de pruebas de OwnTV Fanart. Se instala al lado de la normal.
- 📺 Live player: the programme card shows the whole title (two lines) and up to four lines of synopsis instead of cutting them.
- 🖼️ Live player: the programme picture is loaded as soon as the channel opens and kept, so the info card shows it at once instead of popping it in late.
- 🤾 Handball: Liga ASOBAL with the calendar, results, live score and the table (points and draws). Data comes from the league's own site; a live match is read every half minute.
- 🏎️ Formula 1: a session now finds its channel when the guide calls it "F1" ("Mundial F1"), and the programme shows under Sport on your channels.
- 📺 Live player: the programme card text is a little smaller so the whole title and synopsis fit.
- 🎬 Zapping posters: the programme card adds a quiet line with genre · year · rating and a short synopsis; for films and series the guide leaves blank (e.g. no year), TMDB fills year, rating and synopsis. Next programmes are now two.
- 📋 Channel list: it opens directly on the current channel, with no blink to the top first.
- 🕒 Live player: the programme card now moves on by itself when a programme ends; a channel left open no longer keeps showing the previous one.
- 🎞️ Movies and Series: ◀ from the first column or the search field now goes to the menu instead of opening the categories; they open from the Categories button or a shortcut.
- 📺 Live TV: under the programme picture the synopsis now runs the full width, and up to three coming programmes are listed instead of two.
- ◀ Live TV: moving left across the playlist chips stops at the first one instead of jumping out to the quick channel guide.
- ◀ Movies and Series: ◀ from the first column or the search field now reaches the menu (it was being caught by the category list).
- 🎬 Movies and Series: a little more room between the search bar and the first posters.
- 📺 Live player: the next programme reads "Up next" instead of "Live next", on its own line so it no longer sits crooked.
- ◀ Category sheet: ◀ on the playlist chips moves to the previous chip instead of closing the sheet, and the channels beside it follow the chosen playlist.
- 🌟 Home: the featured slides appear as soon as each one is ready — the first no longer waits for every picture and TMDB lookup to finish.
- 🔁 Quick channel list: a channel tuned from a category's channels makes that category the list ◀ opens next, with the current channel marked.
- 📜 Channel cards: when a focused card's title does not fit, it scrolls smoothly past and starts over.
- ⚡ Menu: moving through the side or top menu opens each section once the cursor rests on it (about half a second), no OK needed. Search, More and Settings still open with OK.
- 🏠 Home: moving back to Home from another section no longer rebuilds its whole feed every time; it refreshes after playback, a profile or playlist change, or after a minute away.
- 🌟 Home featured: right after the first live sport it now shows something worth watching on TV at that moment — a film, premiere, documentary or series — on the HD copy of the channel, leaning to what you usually follow.
- ⭐ Favourites: ordering the favourites of one playlist no longer scrambles the order of the others (channels, movies and series).
- 🎯 TMDB info: the 'Set TMDB name' window has an optional TMDB ID field; with an ID the film or series is matched exactly, with no search by name and year.
- 🗓️ TV Guide: the focused programme cell now stays whole inside the visible timeline, so a long programme that runs past either edge no longer looks cut off or misframed.
- 📝 Programme info: every programme now tries to show a synopsis — when the guide has none, TMDB lends one (with a looser title match). A long synopsis slowly scrolls down and back so all of it can be read.
- 🎚️ Live player: a new 'Quality' button in the controls bar lists the other copies of the channel in the same playlist (FHD, HD, SD…); OK switches to the one you pick. It only appears when the channel has more than one copy.
- ◀ Quick channel strip: ◀ on the first channel goes back to the list.
- 🖼️ Channel list: the programme picture and its text sit higher, no longer pressed against the bottom edge; a long synopsis scrolls slowly.
- 📺 Player banner: the programme card is smaller (picture and text), and a synopsis that does not fit scrolls slowly instead of ending in "…".
- 🔁 Category sheet: switching playlist no longer sends the list down to a group and back up; the list stays where it is while the new playlist loads.
- 🎯 'Set TMDB name' window: the title has its own full-width line and the year and TMDB ID share the one below, so their labels are no longer cut off.
- ⚡ Channel info beside the quick channel strip and list: the programme card now fills in at once when you change channel — schedules are read ahead for the nearby channels and nothing waits before loading, so the text no longer appears piece by piece.
- ✨ New Lumen style (Settings → Style): Lumen Noche (near-black glass) and Lumen Día (pearl glass) with a turquoise accent that no other style uses; the floating menu and panels show as clear glass. The first-run style step now shows the styles in two rows.
- 🔊 Volume card: new "Use for all" button sets the volume you chose as the default for every channel and film that has no volume of its own, so you no longer have to open the card on each one.
- 🏎️ Motorsport: a session now reads "Free practice / Qualifying / Race" with the Grand Prix name under it, instead of two drivers standing in for two teams. It also shows the time, the circuit and the first three (live or final), and the Standings tab lists the drivers' and constructors' championships side by side.
- 🪟 New Home layout "Dock" (Settings › Fanart › Home layout, next to Classic, Cinema and Panel): the focused title large over its picture, and a glass dock below with tabs (Keep watching, Recent and Favorite channels, Sports, Trending, Favorites, New…) — only the rows you have switched on. It works with any colour style.
- 🏍️ MotoGP and other races are now found in the guide by the circuit's place too ("GP de Australia"), not only by the series name, over a wider time window; the sessions read Practice / Warm-up / Qualifying / Sprint / Race.
- 🏟️ Dock Home: matches now show both team crests on the card and large beside the title instead of an empty box
- 🛠️ Fixed the "Delete profile" confirmation closing by itself when opened with a long press
- 🧹 Settings › Video player: the five "Reset saved…" rows are now one "Reset saved choices" row that lets you pick what to forget (or everything)
- 🎵 Two new menu sound styles, "Soft" and "Air": rounded, warm tones and a gentle breath instead of anything metallic
- 🎬 Movies/Series list view: moving down no longer stops when the next title is not ready yet — it retries and steps over it
- 🏠 Dock Home: pressing Up from a card returns to the tab you are on, and a long press on a card offers "Hide this row"
- 🎾 Tennis: players now show their photo (initials stay if it does not load)
- 🐢 The programme/film synopsis that drifts on its own now moves slower and pauses longer at each end; the Dock shows it the same way
- 🖼️ Home layout is now chosen from a small menu: four cards, each with a sketch of how Home looks (Classic, Cinema, Panel, Dock) — no more cycling with Right
- 🏠 Dock: pressing Up from any card now goes back to the tab you are on; a long press on a tab also offers "Hide this row"; the synopsis keeps room under its last line so it is no longer cut
- 📝 Dock: a channel's programme synopsis now shows (read from the guide only for the card you rest on)
- 🏎️ Racing: a film or show with "Formula" in its title is no longer taken for a Grand Prix
- 📺 Channel info banner: when two guide entries overlap, it now shows the one that has a synopsis
- 🏁 Racing classification: the rows can now be reached with Down, so the whole list scrolls into view instead of being cut at the bottom
- 🪶 Light mode: no auto-drifting text, no image fades and a smaller picture cache. It never switches on by itself — if the system reports memory really running out, the app asks once whether to switch it on
- 📺 Live player banner: lower — the synopsis takes two lines and "Next" sits on one line; channel logo and programme picture keep their size
- 🔘 Live TV: ◀ no longer opens the categories — a new "Categories" button next to the guide and sort buttons does
- 👁️ New "Preview with OK" (Settings › Live preview): nothing plays while you move through the list; OK starts the preview, OK again goes full screen.
- 🪶 New "Light mode" switch (Appearance): off unless you switch it on (or say yes when the app notices memory running out)
- 🔊 Menu sounds: a list that plays each style as you move over it (leaving without choosing keeps the one you had)
- ♻️ Appearance: one "Reset appearance" row puts theme, accent, focus highlight, glass and background, text and window sizes and zoom back
- 🎨 Home layout picker: more colour in the sketches, the Classic billboard nearer the middle, and the text stays readable on the highlighted card in light and dark
- 🎵 Menu sounds: the "Air" style is replaced by "Kalimba" — soft plucked notes, with its own sound for move, OK and Back
- 👁️ Preview with OK: the preview now stays on the channel you pressed OK on while you move around; OK on another channel switches it, OK again on the same one opens it full screen
- 🎨 Colour styles: the picker now shows every style drawn as a small Home in its own colours (Lumen Night/Day, Sunset, Studio, Showcase, Pulse, Neon, Snow, Paper, Sand)
- 🖼️ Live TV and Movies & Series layouts: each option is a card with a drawing of how the screen will look
- 🖼️ OK on "Live TV layout", "Movies & Series layout" and "Live TV opens in" now shows the drawings (◀ ▶ still change the choice in place)
- 🏠 Dock: "Hide this row" on Keep watching now really hides it
- ◀ Live TV: from the first card (or the search field) ◀ goes on to the side menu

- 🏁 Formula 1: a finished session's Timeline now lists the pit stops (driver, lap, time) and the race director's notes — safety car, red and chequered flags, penalties — from a free history service. Nothing is asked while a session runs, and only when you open it
