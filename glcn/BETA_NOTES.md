Versión de pruebas de OwnTV Fanart. Se instala al lado de la normal.
- 🎞️ **Trailers in your language first, when there is one**
- 📺 **Channel panel: programme picture → else the channel logo → else just the text; synopsis up to 4 lines**
- ⚡ **Guide pictures appear faster when zapping**
- 🔄 **Automatic sync between your paired devices while the app is open**
- 🔘 **Switch to turn automatic sync on or off**
- 📺 **Watch on TV: send a channel, film or series from your phone to your TV**
- 🧩 **Sync page: PIN and QR card now fits on screen**
- 🏷️ **Platform logos: wide by default, square service icons as an option**
- ⚡ **Platform logo appears faster**
- 🏷️ **Studio name shown when it has no logo**
- 🧭 **Guide matches channels like “ES-4 La 1 HD” or “ES-3 La 1 SD” by itself, on TV and phone**
- 🗂️ **Playlists are a row of chips right under the category search**
- 🙈 **Long-press OK on Recent, Favourites or a playlist to hide it; “Show hidden” brings it back**
- ⚡ **Posters while zapping: no stale poster, and the whole stretch around the cursor is preloaded**
- 📺 **Live player: channel and programme (with poster) on top, all controls in one full-width row**
- 🏟️ **Watch live: only channels airing the match (or about to) — no more “usually airs” rows**
- 🖼️ **Zap panel shows the same programme picture as the guide (no mismatched TMDB image)**
- 🎬 **Home Cinema: rows fade out softly instead of a hard cut when scrolled; Movies/Series use the cover when a title has no wide picture**
- 🖼️ **Live list: bigger channel logos and the programme poster beside its info**
- 🆕 **“New” label on movies and series added in the last week**
- 🎤 **Mic button in TV search: speak with the SHIELD remote (shown only if the TV has voice recognition)**
- 🔢 **Episode numbers readable on light themes (white on the dark badge)**
- 🎞️ **Emby: what you already watched (or half-watched) on the server shows up here — ✓ marks, progress bars and Continue watching**
- 📺 **Channel panel: programme picture → else the channel logo → else just the text; synopsis up to 4 lines**
- 🧹 **Hold OK on Continue movies / Continue series / Recent channels cards to remove them**
- 🗑️ **“Clear favorites” in Favorites (all, Live, Movies or Series — with confirmation)**
- ✅ **✓ on the poster of a series you have watched to the end**
- 🪟 **Poster strip: a programme window on the right — logo, title, time, progress bar, synopsis and what comes next**
- 🗂️ **Choose several playlists at once: tick the ones you want in the playlist picker (OK ticks / unticks) — Live, Guide and Home follow**
- 📋 **Guide: wider channel-name column so names are no longer cut**
- 🗂️ **Category sheet: the channels of the focused category appear beside it with what is on now (▶ enters, OK tunes, ◀ goes back) — and ◀ ▶ over the playlist chips switches list without OK**
- 🔁 **Channel failover: if a channel will not play, it switches to the same channel elsewhere in its playlist (other group or quality), best picture first**
- 🎯 **Live ExoPlayer opens multi-quality channels on the best quality — sharper picture right after a zap**
- 🏀 **ACB: the month calendar reads the whole season list from the feed (not only the next and last 15 games)**
- 🔁 Channel list wraps around: ▲ on the first channel jumps to the last, ▼ on the last to the first.
- 🎯 Categories: moving ◀▶ across the playlist chips no longer drops the cursor down to Favourites.
- 🏀 Basketball: Liga ACB and EuroLeague with the full season calendar, results, live quarter and the table. Data is published by a small scheduled job in a separate repository, so it no longer depends on a free feed that was cut.
- 🔊 ExoPlayer: the sound no longer drops a few seconds in when auto frame rate switches the display.
- ⚡ Zapping list: the schedule column is read ahead for the channels around the cursor, so it no longer appears late.
- 🎯 Home: the Sports row rests at its first card when you come back to it, instead of leaving it cut at the edge.
- 👨‍👩‍👧 Family profile: a kids profile gets a short menu (Home, Live TV, Guide, Movies, Series) and no Settings or More, on top of the adult-content filter it already had. Put a PIN on the adult profiles so the way back is closed.
- 🖼️ Channel panel: the programme picture next to its description is now widescreen and seen whole, instead of a tall crop that cut people off.
- 📺 Live player: the programme card shows the whole title (two lines) and up to four lines of synopsis instead of cutting them.
- 🖼️ Live player: the programme picture is loaded as soon as the channel opens and kept, so the info card shows it at once instead of popping it in late.
- 🤾 Handball: Liga ASOBAL with the calendar, results, live score and the table (points and draws). Data comes from the league's own site; a live match is read every half minute.
- 🏎️ Formula 1: a session now finds its channel when the guide calls it "F1" ("Mundial F1"), and the programme shows under Sport on your channels.
- 📺 Live player: the programme card text is a little smaller so the whole title and synopsis fit.
- 🎬 Zapping posters: the programme card adds a quiet line with genre · year · rating and a short synopsis; for films and series the guide leaves blank (e.g. no year), TMDB fills year, rating and synopsis. Next programmes are now two.
- 📋 Channel list: it opens directly on the current channel, with no blink to the top first.
- 🕒 Live player: the programme card now moves on by itself when a programme ends; a channel left open no longer keeps showing the previous one.
- 🎞️ Movies and Series: ◀ from the first column or the search field now goes to the menu instead of opening the categories; they open from the Categories button or a shortcut.
- 📺 Live TV: under the programme picture the synopsis now runs the full width, and up to three coming programmes are listed instead of two.
- ◀ Live TV: moving left across the playlist chips stops at the first one instead of jumping out to the quick channel guide.
- ◀ Movies and Series: ◀ from the first column or the search field now reaches the menu (it was being caught by the category list).
- 🎬 Movies and Series: a little more room between the search bar and the first posters.
- 📺 Live player: the next programme reads "Up next" instead of "Live next", on its own line so it no longer sits crooked.
- ◀ Category sheet: ◀ on the playlist chips moves to the previous chip instead of closing the sheet, and the channels beside it follow the chosen playlist.
- 🌟 Home: the featured slides appear as soon as each one is ready — the first no longer waits for every picture and TMDB lookup to finish.
- 🔁 Quick channel list: a channel tuned from a category's channels makes that category the list ◀ opens next, with the current channel marked.
- 📜 Channel cards: when a focused card's title does not fit, it scrolls smoothly past and starts over.
- ⚡ Menu: moving through the side or top menu opens each section once the cursor rests on it (about half a second), no OK needed. Search, More and Settings still open with OK.
- 🏠 Home: moving back to Home from another section no longer rebuilds its whole feed every time; it refreshes after playback, a profile or playlist change, or after a minute away.
- 🌟 Home featured: right after the first live sport it now shows something worth watching on TV at that moment — a film, premiere, documentary or series — on the HD copy of the channel, leaning to what you usually follow.
- ⭐ Favourites: ordering the favourites of one playlist no longer scrambles the order of the others (channels, movies and series).
- 🎯 TMDB info: the 'Set TMDB name' window has an optional TMDB ID field; with an ID the film or series is matched exactly, with no search by name and year.
- 🗓️ TV Guide: the focused programme cell now stays whole inside the visible timeline, so a long programme that runs past either edge no longer looks cut off or misframed.
- 📝 Programme info: every programme now tries to show a synopsis — when the guide has none, TMDB lends one (with a looser title match). A long synopsis slowly scrolls down and back so all of it can be read.
- 🎚️ Live player: a new 'Quality' button in the controls bar lists the other copies of the channel in the same playlist (FHD, HD, SD…); OK switches to the one you pick. It only appears when the channel has more than one copy.
