## Beta

Versión de pruebas de OwnTV Fanart. Se instala al lado de la normal.
- 📐 Home (Panel): the featured billboard and the Sports scores now use the whole width ("Sports now" channel cards keep the side panel); the side panel slides in, and the rows make room for it, only when you go down to a row it describes
- 📝 Home (Panel): "Tonight" cards show the programme's synopsis in the side panel
- 🖼️ Home (Panel): the side panel always shows the picture of the card in focus — a card without one no longer shows the previous card's picture
- 📺 Home: "Keep watching" films and series show the show's name, the episode, time left, synopsis and backdrop in the side panel
- 🔁 Programme info: "Airs again this week" also finds the other nights of a daily show whose title changes with each episode (El hormiguero · guests), showing which episode each one is; sport still needs the very same title, so other matches of the same league are never listed
- ⏱️ Programme info: the time line now reads "Ends at 23:00 · 23 min left", so the end time is no longer mistaken for the start
- 🖼️ Live TV (list): the programme picture now sits whole in the space under the preview, instead of a large crop running up behind it
- ✂️ Home: the backdrop's slow zoom no longer spills out beside the menu rail
- 📺 Player: a channel opened from Home no longer shows the previous channel's programme in its info card and channel list (the Live list behind the player could restore its old row and take over)
- 🙈 TV guide: the option now reads "Show channels without guide · Yes / No", and with No the channels with nothing from now on are hidden too (also those whose guide ended yesterday)
- 🕘 Home: Recent channels shows the channel you just watched as soon as you leave the player
- 🤾 Sports: six more handball competitions with calendar, results and table — EHF Champions League and EHF European League (live score from the EHF, tables by group), German Handball-Bundesliga, French StarLigue, Danish Herreligaen and Hungarian K&H Liga. Turn them on in Settings › Sports
- 🎾 Sports › Tennis: the ATP and WTA rankings in full (150 players) with photo, country, points and how far each moved this week; far more players now have a photo everywhere (about 8 in 10 instead of 3 in 10)
- 📈 Settings › Fluency meter: a small box in the corner shows, for each screen, the frames per second, the share of slow frames, the worst frame and the memory in use — to measure smoothness before and after each change
- ⚡ Movies and Series: when focus rests on a title, the backdrops of the titles around it are fetched and decoded ahead, so the next step shows its picture at once
- 🧠 Pictures: the in-memory picture cache grows with the box (twice as large on 3 GB boxes such as the SHIELD; unchanged in light mode), so going back to a row shows its pictures without decoding them again
