## Beta

Versión de pruebas de OwnTV Fanart. Se instala al lado de la normal.

- 📐 Player channel list: set the width of groups, channels and programme in Settings › Layout › Panel widths (new "Player channel list" row). The groups fold to their icons while you browse the channels, and the programme column takes that room.
- 🪟 Player channel list: the programme column is as solid as the list, so the schedule no longer mixes with the picture or its subtitles.
- 📺 Cine and Ficha banners: the stream's facts (resolution, Hz, HDR…) are shown next to the channel, as in the other styles.
- 🖼️ Cine banner: the programme's picture beside the title.
- 🎮 Remote shortcuts: new "Programme info" action — opens the programme on air in full (picture, synopsis, what follows) with one button.
- 🖼️ Live TV, Ficha layout: with a wallpaper, photo or gradient as background, the programme picture now shows inside the panel behind the text (blurred and dimmed).
- 📡 Channel logos: a channel your list brings without a logo (a tuner's channels, a brand-new one) now takes the guide's picon instead of the generic TV icon.
- 🌅 New launch screen: the icon's sunset as a wide landscape with the logo, instead of the icon on black; it fades into the app once it is ready.
- 🎬 Featured: recently added films you have already watched (or started) no longer appear.
- 🏷️ Channel logos that come on a plain white square (e.g. La Séptima) lose that backdrop and sit on the tile like the others.
- 🎞️ Trailers: a title with no trailer in Spanish or English (a Polish or Korean series…) now gets the one in its own language; titles saved without a trailer are checked again.
- 💬 Full-screen trailers in another language (English, Polish…) play with Spanish subtitles when the video has them; Spanish ones play clean.
- 🎡 Live TV: holding ▲/▼ turns the channel list like a wheel — it glides on the screen's frames, speeds up gently while held, the highlight stays put and the rows slide under it; let go and it rolls on to the next channel and settles.
- 🖼️ Programme info (ⓘ): the live picture now sits exactly in its frame with any UI zoom or safe area (it drifted off on some TVs).
- ✂️ Player bar: the channel's facts (1080p, fps, Mbps…) show only as whole chips — those that do not fit are left out instead of cut — and the programme beside takes at most about half the row.
- 📺 Player channel list (◀ while watching): the picture slides aside and shrinks into the space beside the list, still playing — nothing covers it any more.
- 🔑 Emby/Jellyfin: the app keeps its sign-in across restarts, so the server no longer lists a new login every time the app opens.
- 🧹 Keep watching: a film or show you take out stays out, even if your Emby/Jellyfin server still has it half-watched (it comes back if you watch it again).
- 🛠️ Fixed a crash while watching live TV: the channel list's programme read could run past the end of a list that had just changed group.
- 📺 Player channel list: the picture keeps one size — half the screen, up on the right — whatever the list shows; the programme is told under it, and the groups open over the channels instead of pushing the picture aside.
- 🎨 Player channel list, new look: under the picture, the programme on air with its time left, progress and a short synopsis, then the next three programmes as picture cards; the focused programme's picture softly fills the page behind.
- 🗂️ Player channel list: no more column of initials — ◀ opens the categories over the channels with their full names, counts and, with several playlists, each playlist's coloured tag.
- 🌊 Smoother scrolling everywhere: lists, rows and grids now glide on a spring that keeps its speed — they set off gently, flow under a held key and slow down softly instead of stopping dead.
- 🎡 Live TV fast scroll (hold ▲/▼): starts from rest, keeps an even pace, and on release coasts a few channels before settling; the rows hold their programme info while turning so it no longer stutters.
- 🔄 Merged OwnTV 5.2.2 (thanks ahXN00 and contributors): Settings lock with its own PIN and protected profiles, local auto-sync between paired devices, title art and 4K badge in the player, series page title art, "Record every showing" can be stopped from Recordings, "Show likely channels" in Sports and better competition matching.
- 🖼️ Player channel list: a bigger picture, and what the focused channel is showing appears as a picture under it, with the programme in light text over it; the page behind the list stays plain.
- 🎡 Player channel list: hold ▲/▼ to turn the list like a wheel — even pace, a soft coast when you let go, and the panel under the picture waits until it stops.
- 📐 Player channel list: the picture now takes all the width beside the list; the programme's picture sits between it and the foot of the screen, with the programme (title, time left, synopsis) on the left and what follows on the right — ▶ walks through it.
- 🌫️ New wallpaper: "Bruma azul" (teal haze), in Wallpapers › Dark.
- 🃏 Player channel list: the picture and a dark card under it form one piece; the programme's picture shows whole at the card's right, genres appear as a tag and the synopsis drops the guide's "(Genre)" and "País:" clutter.
- ↕️ Fast scrolling: the wheel is gone from Live TV and the player's channel list — holding ▲/▼ steps channel by channel again, gliding on the new smooth scrolling; the panel under the picture waits until you let go.
- ℹ️ Programme info: a clean synopsis kept to the left half, genres, year, length and rating as tags, the buttons right under the synopsis, the right-hand panels stacked under the picture without gaps, and a simple live dot on the picture.
- 🎯 Focused rows: a switch or value on the right now reads clearly on the highlighted row, in light and dark themes.
- ⬆️ Player channel list: ▲ on the first programme of "Up next" closes the list and gives the picture back.
- 🚀 Launch: the plain icon screen before the launch picture is gone — the app opens straight onto the sunset launch screen.
- 🖼️ Player channel list: walking "Up next", the card's picture follows the picked programme.
- ✨ Sections now come in with a short fade and a slight rise instead of appearing all at once.
- 🎯 The focus box travels: jumping from the top bar to a row, or from a menu to its page, the highlight glides over from where it was instead of switching off in one place and on in another.
- 🌤️ Three new light wallpapers: Bruma perla, Amanecer pastel and Arena (Wallpapers › Light).
- ✔️ The watched check on posters stays visible with a white accent and on the focused poster.
- 📂 Player channel list: the categories panel is wider so names read whole, and a ‹ before the group's name shows that ◀ opens them.
- 🔒 **A channel that won't open says why:** a locked category or a channel not allowed on the profile shows a message instead of OK doing nothing, and the reason goes into the playback report; a channel left on a category a sync replaced opens from its current one
- 🔍 **Player bar at any interface size:** when the buttons don't fit (a larger interface size), each group scrolls as you move instead of buttons being cut off; EXO/MPV stays on one line, and subtitles rise above the bar while the controls are up
- 🩺 Playlists: each row shows its health at a glance — a green, amber or red dot, "Updated 2 hours ago", the days left before it expires (amber under 15) and, after a failed update, the reason in plain words with Try again.
- 🧰 Playlists: fewer buttons — Edit, Update, More options and Delete; Status and connection, Turn off, Colour and tag and Clean names live under More options.
- ⏸️ Playlists can be turned off without deleting them: kept, but hidden everywhere and not updated until turned back on.
- 🎨 Each playlist's tag and colour can be chosen (Colour and tag); they show wherever rows are marked by playlist.
- ⏰ A one-time notice a week before an Xtream subscription expires, with its date.
- 🧭 Settings: each group shows the everyday settings first; the rest wait behind "Show advanced settings" (it says how many and which), and each group remembers whether you left it open.
- 🖼️ Settings: Fanart settings also appear in the group they belong to (Appearance, Layout, Player…) with a FANART tag; the Fanart group still lists them all.
- 🆕 Settings: a NEW badge marks settings added or changed in this version, on the row and on its group's card; it goes away once you open it.
- 🏠 Settings: Home background is one row now — picture, your own image and dimming in a single popup.
- 🔤 Spanish: "Categories" and "Channels ocultos" in Customize now read "Categorías" and "Canales ocultos".
- 📋 Settings: the cards under More › Settings show your current values (playlists and guide days, theme · accent · size, players, auto frame rate, languages, live preview) instead of a fixed description.
- 😴 Sleep timer: it keeps counting when you leave the player and the channel carries on in the preview pane or in Multiview — before, it cancelled itself three seconds later.
- 🌘 Sleep timer: the last minute dims the picture and says so; any button gives 15 more minutes.
- ⬛ Sleep timer: when it stops playback, a black screen stays until a button is pressed, and the screen is no longer kept awake, so the TV's own sleep takes over even where "turn off the screen" isn't available.
- 🏷️ **Opening a channel shows its own name at once:** the card over the video no longer keeps the previous channel's name, logo and quality marks until the new picture arrives
- 🔔 White or light accents: the reminder bell on Home cards, the watched check on wide cards and the chosen mark in popups no longer vanish (a dark disc or the accent's ink instead of white on white).
- ⚡ Brisker everywhere: menus, the focus glide, scrolling, popups and transitions run at 0.6× their old length — the snappy feel of a SHIELD with its animation scale at 0.5x — while the slow backdrop drifts and Ken Burns keep their calm. With Android's animations off, they stay off.
- 📺 Recent channels on the TV's home: each card now carries the programme's start and end, the channel's own logo and the fanart served from the app itself, so launchers with no internet of their own (such as Clean Your TV) can show the picture and a progress bar; the row also refreshes a minute after a programme ends.
- ⏩ Live: when a server answers a channel with an empty stream (an overloaded or rate-limiting restreamer), the player moves on to mpv after 2.5 s instead of waiting the full 12 s.
- 🚀 Smoother right after an update: the rules that ask Android to compile the app's own screens ahead were written wrongly and matched nothing; fixed, and they now cover the player and core too.
- 🧯 Guide and list previews: when the SHIELD is critically short of memory and the preview shows nothing (or drops more frames than it shows), it is let go instead of insisting — what froze the Guide once. Moving the focus tunes again.
- 🃏 Home: the landscape cards pop as they take focus — they grow a little with a small bounce, the focus box with them, and draw over their neighbours, like a launcher's app tiles. Posters stay as they are.
- 🪀 TV en directo and the Guide: the channel list moves with a little give when it scrolls (a soft spring instead of a dead stop), without overdoing it.
- ✅ Home: films you have already watched (their ✓, or marked watched) no longer appear in Recent films or the Featured billboard.
- 🎯 TV en directo: the channel highlight now slides from row to row with a little give (a soft spring), instead of jumping; in the Guide, ▲/▼ between channels lands the same way.
- 🔕 The "Rebuilding sports…" note no longer pops up on every screen: the sports rebuild runs on its own in the background (its progress stays in Sports › Settings), and only a failure is said.
- 🪀 More spring: the channel highlight in TV en directo and the Guide now passes the row by about an eighth and settles back (was a slight give), the channel list scrolls with a clearer give, and Home's landscape cards pop a little more (1.08).
- 🌤️ Home cards lift: the focused landscape card throws a deeper shadow that grows with its pop, and its title and line come forward (brighter, settling a little lower) while the others stay a step back.
- 🔍 A title chosen on Home grows into its page (a short zoom and fade) instead of arriving like any other section.
- 🫧 Side menu: each icon gives a little springy hop as the focus reaches it.
- 🔈 **Audio tracks your device can't play are marked** "not supported on this device" and are never forced on: picking one used to break the channel, and every later visit too. If a picked track still fails, the player goes back to the default one and forgets the pick
- 🧩 **A decoder that refuses a channel mid-stream gives up after two tries** instead of eight, and the playback report names the refused format
- ⏩ Channel list over the picture: holding ▲/▼ speeds up (one row, then 3, then 6 at a time) and stops where you let go; typing a channel number on the remote jumps to it (or the next one after it), with the number shown a moment.
- 🎞️ Trakt (Settings › Sources): sign in on the TV with a code typed at trakt.tv/activate; each profile can have its own account. Films and episodes you finish (or mark watched) go to your Trakt history — except those from Emby/Jellyfin, which the server's own Trakt plugin already reports. Films watched on Trakt get their ✓ here, and a new Home row, "My Trakt watchlist", shows the watchlist films that are in your playlists. Only titles the app has matched to TMDB are sent or marked.
- 🌫️ Home: the row peeking in at the foot of the screen now dissolves into the page instead of showing cut-off cards, and a row brought down from above keeps its heading clear of the top bar.
- 🔢 The scroll bar's channel-number bubble in TV en directo is readable with a white or light accent (it showed an empty white pill).
- 🫥 Live cards (zapping strip, Home rows): the channel's logo shows large, grey and faint behind the words on the right, half off the edge — a hint of whose card it is that the text reads straight over.
- 👻 More faint "ghost" details: the Guide shows the channel's logo in grey behind the programme panel; in TV en directo the focused channel row carries its logo faintly on the right; a series page shows the season ("T2") huge and in outline behind the episodes; a film's page shows its year the same way behind the words; and each sports match card has both teams' crests, grey and faint, half off its two edges.
- ✂️ Home: a row only partly on screen — cut by the foot of the page or by the top bar — now fades as a whole instead of showing cut-off cards, and comes back as it slides fully in.
- 🔁 TV en directo, Cinema layout: ▶ on the last channel goes round to the first; with the menu on top, ◀ on the first goes round to the last (with the side menu, ◀ there still opens the menu). Holding the key still stops at the end.
- 🕗 Guide: the times of the focused programme are readable with every accent colour (they were dark grey on a light focus, under a white title); they now take the title's colour, a little softer.
