## Beta

Versión de pruebas de OwnTV Fanart. Se instala al lado de la normal.

- 📐 Player channel list: set the width of groups, channels and programme in Settings › Layout › Panel widths (new "Player channel list" row). The groups fold to their icons while you browse the channels, and the programme column takes that room.
- 🪟 Player channel list: the programme column is as solid as the list, so the schedule no longer mixes with the picture or its subtitles.
- 📺 Cine and Ficha banners: the stream's facts (resolution, Hz, HDR…) are shown next to the channel, as in the other styles.
- 🖼️ Cine banner: the programme's picture beside the title.
- 🎮 Remote shortcuts: new "Programme info" action — opens the programme on air in full (picture, synopsis, what follows) with one button.
- 🖼️ Live TV, Ficha layout: with a wallpaper, photo or gradient as background, the programme picture now shows inside the panel behind the text (blurred and dimmed).
- 📡 Channel logos: a channel your list brings without a logo (a tuner's channels, a brand-new one) now takes the guide's picon instead of the generic TV icon.
- 🌅 New launch screen: the icon's sunset as a wide landscape with the logo, instead of the icon on black; it fades into the app once it is ready.
- 🎬 Featured: recently added films you have already watched (or started) no longer appear.
- 🏷️ Channel logos that come on a plain white square (e.g. La Séptima) lose that backdrop and sit on the tile like the others.
- 🎞️ Trailers: a title with no trailer in Spanish or English (a Polish or Korean series…) now gets the one in its own language; titles saved without a trailer are checked again.
- 💬 Full-screen trailers in another language (English, Polish…) play with Spanish subtitles when the video has them; Spanish ones play clean.
- 🎡 Live TV: holding ▲/▼ turns the channel list like a wheel — it glides on the screen's frames, speeds up gently while held, the highlight stays put and the rows slide under it; let go and it rolls on to the next channel and settles.
- 🖼️ Programme info (ⓘ): the live picture now sits exactly in its frame with any UI zoom or safe area (it drifted off on some TVs).
- ✂️ Player bar: the channel's facts (1080p, fps, Mbps…) show only as whole chips — those that do not fit are left out instead of cut — and the programme beside takes at most about half the row.
- 📺 Player channel list (◀ while watching): the picture slides aside and shrinks into the space beside the list, still playing — nothing covers it any more.
- 🔑 Emby/Jellyfin: the app keeps its sign-in across restarts, so the server no longer lists a new login every time the app opens.
- 🧹 Keep watching: a film or show you take out stays out, even if your Emby/Jellyfin server still has it half-watched (it comes back if you watch it again).
- 🛠️ Fixed a crash while watching live TV: the channel list's programme read could run past the end of a list that had just changed group.
- 📺 Player channel list: the picture keeps one size — half the screen, up on the right — whatever the list shows; the programme is told under it, and the groups open over the channels instead of pushing the picture aside.
- 🎨 Player channel list, new look: under the picture, the programme on air with its time left, progress and a short synopsis, then the next three programmes as picture cards; the focused programme's picture softly fills the page behind.
- 🗂️ Player channel list: no more column of initials — ◀ opens the categories over the channels with their full names, counts and, with several playlists, each playlist's coloured tag.
- 🌊 Smoother scrolling everywhere: lists, rows and grids now glide on a spring that keeps its speed — they set off gently, flow under a held key and slow down softly instead of stopping dead.
- 🎡 Live TV fast scroll (hold ▲/▼): starts from rest, keeps an even pace, and on release coasts a few channels before settling; the rows hold their programme info while turning so it no longer stutters.
- 🔄 Merged OwnTV 5.2.2 (thanks ahXN00 and contributors): Settings lock with its own PIN and protected profiles, local auto-sync between paired devices, title art and 4K badge in the player, series page title art, "Record every showing" can be stopped from Recordings, "Show likely channels" in Sports and better competition matching.
- 🖼️ Player channel list: a bigger picture, and what the focused channel is showing appears as a picture under it, with the programme in light text over it; the page behind the list stays plain.
- 🎡 Player channel list: hold ▲/▼ to turn the list like a wheel — even pace, a soft coast when you let go, and the panel under the picture waits until it stops.
