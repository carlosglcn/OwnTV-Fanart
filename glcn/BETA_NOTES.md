## Beta

Versión de pruebas de OwnTV Fanart. Se instala al lado de la normal.
- 📐 Home (Panel): the featured billboard and the Sports scores now use the whole width ("Sports now" channel cards keep the side panel); the side panel slides in, and the rows make room for it, only when you go down to a row it describes
- 📝 Home (Panel): "Tonight" cards show the programme's synopsis in the side panel
- 🖼️ Home (Panel): the side panel always shows the picture of the card in focus — a card without one no longer shows the previous card's picture
- 📺 Home: "Keep watching" films and series show the show's name, the episode, time left, synopsis and backdrop in the side panel
- 🔁 Programme info: "Airs again this week" also finds the other nights of a daily show whose title changes with each episode (El hormiguero · guests), showing which episode each one is; sport still needs the very same title, so other matches of the same league are never listed
- ⏱️ Programme info: the time line now reads "Ends at 23:00 · 23 min left", so the end time is no longer mistaken for the start
- 🖼️ Live TV (list): the programme picture now sits whole in the space under the preview, instead of a large crop running up behind it
- ✂️ Home: the backdrop's slow zoom no longer spills out beside the menu rail
- 📺 Player: a channel opened from Home no longer shows the previous channel's programme in its info card and channel list (the Live list behind the player could restore its old row and take over)
- 🙈 TV guide: the option now reads "Show channels without guide · Yes / No", and with No the channels with nothing from now on are hidden too (also those whose guide ended yesterday)
- 🕘 Home: Recent channels shows the channel you just watched as soon as you leave the player
- 🤾 Sports: six more handball competitions with calendar, results and table — EHF Champions League and EHF European League (live score from the EHF, tables by group), German Handball-Bundesliga, French StarLigue, Danish Herreligaen and Hungarian K&H Liga. Turn them on in Settings › Sports
- 🎾 Sports › Tennis: the ATP and WTA rankings in full (150 players) with photo, country, points and how far each moved this week; far more players now have a photo everywhere (about 8 in 10 instead of 3 in 10)
- 📈 Settings › Fluency meter: a small box in the corner shows, for each screen, the frames per second, the share of slow frames, the worst frame and the memory in use — to measure smoothness before and after each change
- ⚡ Movies and Series: when focus rests on a title, the backdrops of the titles around it are fetched and decoded ahead, so the next step shows its picture at once
- 🧠 Pictures: the in-memory picture cache grows with the box (twice as large on 3 GB boxes such as the SHIELD; unchanged in light mode), so going back to a row shows its pictures without decoding them again
- 🩺 More › Error log › Export now also says how the app last closed (Android 11+): a freeze the system closed, with what each part of the app was doing at that moment, a crash inside a player engine, or the system reclaiming memory — the cases that used to leave no trace
- 🌍 Sports: follow the usual competitions of a country with one press — from the empty Sports page ("Follow the usual for Spain", or another country) or in Settings › Sports › Leagues of a country. It only adds; nothing you follow is removed
- 🚴 Sports › Cycling: the UCI WorldTour — every one-day race and every stage of the stage races, with route, distance, profile, start time and the podium once it is over; the general classification (with time gaps) of the stage race on now or the last one run. Spanish names for the big races so the guide finds their broadcasts (La Vuelta, Tour de Francia, Giro de Italia…)
- 🎱 Sports › Snooker: the World Snooker Tour — the matches of the events on now and coming up (round, best of, time), results, and the world ranking. Country presets now include cycling and snooker where they are followed
- 🔄 Merged the latest from the original OwnTV (thanks, ahXN00 and its contributors):
  - 🗓️ TV guide: smoother scrolling, the now-line runs to the bottom, the Rewind mark on catch-up channels, Auto-match when a new guide matches no channel, and guide priority with Move up / Move down in EPG sources
  - 🧹 Playlists: a Names option to clean channel names, Resync now in the playlist menu, and more retries when a list download fails
  - 🎙️ Search: voice search through the remote's microphone (the original's button; saying "pon" or "busca" still works)
  - ▶️ Player buttons in Remote shortcuts: what a short and a held OK do with the controls hidden, and flipping CH±/▲▼. A held OK still opens the channel list by default
  - 🚀 Startup: open the guide or Live TV on the last channel
  - 🏷️ Navigation: a Menu labels choice (off, when focused, always)
  - 🏠 Home: the last card of a row stays on screen when it has focus; the update popup's What's new scrolls with the remote
  - 🗃️ Faster playlist and guide writes (fewer database indexes)
- 🗂️ Player: ◀ on a live channel opens the new zap panel, in the style of TiviMate but with the app's look and theme — groups (and your playlists above them), the channels with logo, number, programme on air and progress, and the focused channel's picture or schedule on the right. It opens on the playing channel; one cursor glides between rows; ◀ goes to the groups (their channels show as you move), ▲ from the first group reaches the playlists (whole names, the row slides), OK tunes, ▶ or Back closes. The right column follows the zap view chosen in Settings (picture or schedule). A channel opened from Home shows its whole group, not the Home row it came from
- ⏪ Player: ◀ now always opens that panel; to rewind, press OK and use ◀ on the bar, or the remote's rewind button
- 💬 About: the OwnTV Fanart Telegram group with its QR code (t.me/owntvfanart), next to the original project's
- ✋ Player: holding OK opens what it is set to (the zap panel by default) as soon as it has been held long enough, instead of only when it is released
- ⚡ Zap panel: the focused channel's picture appears at once when moving — the programmes and pictures of the channels around the cursor are read and decoded ahead
- 🖼️ TV guide pictures: when a guide offers a wide picture for a programme (XMLTV <image type="backdrop">, a still or a landscape icon), it is used instead of the upright poster, whose printed title used to clash with the programme's own title. Applies from the next guide update
- ¹² TV guide and zap panels: a channel's feed number written as a superscript (M+ Terror ¹, M+ Terror ²) is kept in its name, so two feeds of one channel no longer look like the same row
- 🙈 TV guide: switching "Show channels without guide" to No hides them at once, without opening the guide again
- 🔀 TV guide: turning on another playlist (from the playlist menu) links its channels to the guide by name straight away, and if it still has no guide (a playlist added after the guide was last read), the guide is read again by itself — no more "Without guide" until a manual sync
- 🏷️ Movies and Series: quality badges read from the title — on the posters the best picture mark (DV in gold, HDR10+, HDR10, HDR, else 4K) and REMUX (also when written as [4K R] / [DV R]); on the title's page every mark (resolution, REMUX, HDR, Atmos/7.1/5.1)
- 🧈 Home: moving from row to row no longer rebuilds every row and card on the screen — each focus move used to redraw the whole of Home (the jolts between rows); now only the backdrop picture changes
- 🎞️ Home and Sports rows: a row always comes to rest with a whole card at its start, and nothing of the card before it peeks out on the left; the row glides by whole cards, keeping a card of room on each side of the focus
- 🔁 Zap panel: with both the picture and the schedule views on (Settings › Zapping list views), ▶ switches the right column between them; with one only, ▶ closes the panel. The posters view is no longer part of it
- ▶️ Player: the play/pause button sits a little higher, clear of the top edge of the programme card
- 📐 Home (Dock): every card opens wide when it has focus — also those with no wide picture (often the last ones of a row), which now show their poster whole in the middle of the open card
- 🎨 About: the OwnTV Fanart Telegram QR is now drawn like Telegram's own group codes — rounded dots in the Fanart colours, the Telegram plane in the middle and @OWNTVFANART below
- 🌐 About: the languages button opens Settings › App, where the language is (it opened Sound and subtitles)
- 🗓️ Playlist menu (the playlist pill at the top): "Refresh guide" reads every guide again now, without re-reading the playlists — the sync pill shows it working
- ⏩ Live TV with "Pause and rewind live TV" on: going from the preview to full screen and back on the same channel no longer jumps back to an earlier point — it carries on live
- 🪟 Zap panel: the column on the right (picture or schedule) is on a lighter glass, so the TV shows through beside the list; groups and channels keep the full glass so they always read
- 🌌 Ambient glow (Night theme, glass off): the slow pulse is now Aurora — three soft fields of light from your accent drifting slowly behind the screen, as if it were lit from behind; still in light mode or with animations off
- 🎞️ Home rows: the strip of the card before the first one no longer shows at the left edge (the clip now stops just short of the gap)
- ✋ Player: holding OK opens the quick guide again (the channel list with its picture, schedule and posters views; ▶ switches view and it is remembered), while ◀ opens the zap panel; ▶ or Back closes the zap panel
- 🖼️ TV guide with several guides: the guide that wins (the higher one in EPG sources) keeps the picture and synopsis another guide has for the same programme, so the fanart no longer disappears
- 🖼️ TV guide: programme pictures (fanart) are stored again on every guide update — the faster guide writer from the original had been leaving them out. The next guide update rewrites the guide once to bring them back
- 🎾 Tennis: the Standings tab now shows the full ATP/WTA ranking (it used to come up empty), and a new Draw tab lists the tournament round by round with sets and winners.
- 🪟 Zap panel (◀): the whole panel is now see-through (the list a little less than the detail, so it still reads) and every column is narrower, so more of the picture shows
- 🧈 Zap panel (◀): smoother up/down scrolling — the list only lays itself out again when it passes a whole row; the rest of the glide is drawn
- 📺 Sports › Watch live: channels showing the match are ordered by how surely their guide names it (both players or teams first), then by picture quality (4K, FHD, HD, SD)
- 🖼️ Quick guide (hold OK): the programme picture shows at once — what is on is kept once read, like the ◀ panel, instead of being read again on each row
- 🧈 Zap panel (◀): only the row that gains or loses the cursor is redrawn, and the glide is a touch softer, so fast scrolling stays smooth
- ☰ Player picture strip: hold OK on a card for the channel's options — the same menu as Live TV (synopsis, record, schedule, external player, favourite, rename, hide, EPG match and offset, catch-up)
- 🎬 Live TV Cinematic with the menu on top: the hero and rows move up into the room the bar frees, so "You may like" is whole again
- 🔤 Live TV Cinematic: title and synopsis sit on a soft wash of the page colour and the synopsis is narrower, so they read over bright pictures
- 📝 Player picture strip: the floating card now shows the programme's synopsis from the guide (the strip reads its rows without it, so it is fetched for the focused one)
- 🖼️ Films and series with a TMDB id typed by hand: the TMDB poster replaces the provider's on every card (rows, grid, Continue watching, runway) — a hand fix means the provider's data, cover included, was wrong. It stays after re-syncs
- 🃏 Dock: channel cards show the channel's logo whole in the middle over its programme's picture (dimmed); opening the card brings the picture up sharp. No picture: the logo on a tint, never a cropped giant logo. Films and series keep their vertical poster, opening onto the wide picture
- ⚡ Seasonal decorations (leaves, bats, snow…) draw in their own layer, so their animation no longer makes the whole screen redraw on every frame
- 🧈 Scrolling between rows: a gentle ease over about half a second (soft start and landing); ▲ no longer adds its own scroll when the row above is already partly on screen. The spring tried before is gone — with ▼ held the page stopped following the focus
- 🌐 About › languages opens Settings on the Language row, not on the first row of the page
- ⚽ Sports: Ourense CF is named as such (ESPN calls it "Auriense"), so its matches also find their channel
- ✨ Channel options over the player (hold OK on a strip card): a see-through sheet slides in from the right, leaving the picture in view — the programme on air at its head (picture, title, times, progress), then the channel and the options
