## Beta

Versión de pruebas de OwnTV Fanart. Se instala al lado de la normal.
- 🔄 Merged the original app's latest (Nov 19–21): catch-up decodes in hardware without dropped frames and reopens by itself when a programme drops them, a per-playlist "Prefer HLS for catch-up" switch, subtitles from a folder ("Search in folder", best match first, optional auto-load), search and A–Z in every alphabet, a notice while the database is converted after an update, per-profile text sizes and recent searches, voice search with the default app, Ukrainian and Central Kurdish. Thanks, ahXN00.
- ⏳ Settings › Playlists: a playlist waiting its turn to sync says "Queued" instead of "Syncing" — with several updating at once they all read the same and looked stuck.
- 🔎 Player channel list: a quick search. ▲ from the first channel reaches a magnifier beside the list's name; OK opens it with the TV's keyboard, and what you type finds channels in every active playlist as you write — each result says which group it is in, OK tunes it and zapping goes on in that group. Back puts the search away. (▲ on the first channel no longer jumps to the last one.)
- 📌 Live TV: an optional channel list with fixed focus (Settings › Layout › "Channel list with fixed focus", off by default) — the highlight stays at one height and the channels pass under it, as in TiviMate or Apple TV; near the first and last channels it walks to them.
- 🔄 Merged the original app's latest (Nov 21 evening): a channel's **Archive** page with its past days and their programmes, catch-up on Flussonic servers from the VOD playlist, the catch-up guide fills from the playlist when the feed is stale, guide matches re-sync the feed when needed, a Player buttons option for what ▲ ▼ do in the live player (zap, controls and recent channels, or nothing), hold OK / Menu on Home's trending posters, Downloads opens on Recordings, the Info key opens Program details in Live TV. Thanks, ahXN00.
- 🎨 **Posters and backdrops in full colour:** pictures were kept with fewer colours to save memory, which showed as grain and banding in dark posters; now only low-RAM boxes and Light mode keep the saving
- 🔤 **Crisper small type on 720p screens:** many budget TVs draw apps at 720p, where small bold text came out jagged; there it is drawn a step lighter
- 🧹 **No channel watermark in the Guide:** the faint channel logo behind the programme's words is gone
- 🔥 **A new style, Brasa:** warm graphite, an accent that runs from amber to coral, and an outlined focus — a focused row, card or poster is framed in the gradient instead of filled, so the picture under it still shows; buttons and menu items fill with the gradient. First in the style list, so a new install opens on it; who already has the app is offered it once ("Try it"), with a second question to keep it or go back.
- 🔠 Home's row labels (and "Keep watching" in Films and Series) are small spaced capitals instead of heavy titles: the page reads more ordered and the pictures keep the weight. In every style.
- 📚 **Films and Series: a new layout, Shelves** (Settings › Layout, with the others) — Cinematic, with rows above the posters of All: **Popular now** (the titles of Home's Trending that your lists carry), **Recent releases** (this year's, the latest added first) and **Best rated**. Each title once even when a list has it in several qualities; a row with fewer than four titles is left out. Wide pictures where the titles have them, posters where they do not. The section opens on the first shelf, OK and hold OK do what they do on a poster, and coming back from a title returns to its shelf. All the posters follow below as always.
- 🌅 **A light style with the gradient, Alba:** Brasa's sister for daytime — warm ivory, an accent from orange to raspberry and the same outlined focus.
- 🔔 **Menu sounds: a new style, Ember ("Brasa")** — warm and low: a soft knock to move, a rising fifth for OK, the same falling away for Back.
- 🧭 **Top bar: five shapes to choose from** (Settings › Layout › "Top bar shape"; each press moves on to the next and the bar above changes at once). **Capsule** is the bar as it was. **Island** sits in the middle with no background until the focus is on it — the open section carries its icon and a line in the accent, the logo stays at the left. **Tabs**: bare names in larger type over a soft veil, the open one underlined. **Pills**: every section its own chip, the open one light. **Icons**: a short capsule of icons that name themselves when open or focused. Who already had the app keeps the capsule; a new install opens on the island.
- 🧭 **Side menu: four shapes to choose from** (Settings › Layout › "Side menu shape", each press moves on to the next). **Glass** is the menu as it was. **Fade**: the glass thins out into the picture, the open section is marked with a bar in the accent, the focused row is outlined and — once the focus rests on it — shows a larger picture of what waits there; the profile rides beside the logo. **Column**: names alone, large, no icons or counts. **Label**: the menu stays closed and the focused icon says its name and count in a label beside it. Who already had the app keeps Glass; a new install opens on Fade.
- 🩹 Brasa and Alba: on a focused Settings row the value at its right (the chosen option, the switch, the NEW / FANART mark) vanished into the outlined row — it now keeps its own colours there. Side menu "Label": the label no longer stays behind when the focus goes on to the profile.
- 🏟️ **Sports: match cards in the teams' colours.** Each card takes its two sides' colours, read from their crests — home's from the left, away's from the right: deep tones under a dark style, a pale wash of the colours under a light one (races and players keep the plain card). A match about to start says how long is left ("in 29 min · 21:30") in the accent for its last three hours. And when a channel was already found for a match (its page was opened before), the card's foot names it instead of the stadium.
- 🎬 **TV Guide: filter by type.** A new "Type" button in the Guide's header: Everything, or only the channels showing a film, sport, a series, a documentary, the news or something for children **right now**, each with how many channels show it. It works together with the category and the order. The kind comes from what the guide says of each programme; when the last one of a kind ends, the filter lets go by itself.
- 🕒 Date, time & weather colours: a colour picked by hand that would not read under the current style (a pale one on a light style, a dark one on a dark style) gives way to the style's own colour there; the pick is kept and comes back with a style where it reads.
- 🩹 Light styles (Alba, Lumen Día, Nieve…): the player's LIVE badge was pale red words on a pale red wash and could not be read on the light banner — the words are now a deep red there.
- 🎽 Sports cards, the colours put right: a side's colour is now **its own** — the one its feed gives for it (its shirt's), not whichever colour was strongest in its crest, which put yellow on Barcelona and blue on Villarreal. Black-and-white sides read as silver or near black; the crest only stands in where the feed gives no colour (the first time, cards may still show the old colours until the scores refresh).
- 🎾 **Tennis: the surface.** A tennis card says what the tournament is played on — clay, grass, hard court, indoor hard court — after its name, and takes that surface's colour (orange, green, blue, violet). Told from the tournament's name; one that is not on the list shows nothing.
- 🏆 Sports cards: the **competition's own logo** before its name — LaLiga, the Premier League, the Champions League… — as the scores' feed gives it (its version for a dark background under a dark style). Tennis shows the **tournament's own logo** for the big ones (the Grand Slams, the Masters 1000, the Finals, the Davis Cup…), from our own data file, and the tour's (ATP, WTA) for the rest.
- 🎽 Sports cards, the colours rethought: the card itself is plain again, and each side carries **a bar of its own colour beside its crest** — vivid, as it is, never dulled into a background (Villarreal's yellow had turned mustard). Tennis players carry their tournament's surface colour.
- ⚡ **Brisk response** (Settings › Layout, on by default): everything that moves after a key — the focus sliding to its new place, lists and the Home page gliding, menus and pages opening — now takes half the time it did, and the menu opens the section the focus rests on after 0.2 s instead of 0.4. The slow ambient motion (pictures drifting) is untouched. Switch it off to get the gentler pace back and compare.
- 🏍️ **MotoGP: standings and results.** A MotoGP session's page now has the **riders' championship** (the Table tab, with points) and, once a session has run — practice, qualifying, the sprint, the race — its **result**: every rider in order, with the winner's time and the others' gap. Both come from the championship's own results feed.
